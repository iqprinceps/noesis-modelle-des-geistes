# EP16 — Expanded Asset Research 02

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Purpose:** second research pass beyond the initial verified manifest. This pass prioritizes additional institutional repositories, full early-book scans, scientific diagrams, historical censorship iconography, and rights-clean occult imagery.

## What this pass adds
The first pass established the core 1564/1758/1835 Index volumes, Galileo/Descartes portraits, the 1632 *Dialogo*, Agrippa 1533, and the 1966 Vatican documents. This second pass adds several stronger alternatives and deeper visual branches:

1. a Library of Congress **1632 autograph working manuscript** of Galileo’s *Dialogo* with 128 images and a World Digital Library reuse statement;
2. Galileo’s **1613 sunspot book** and high-resolution sunspot imagery;
3. Descartes’ complete **1637 Discours de la méthode** and **1644 Principia philosophiae** scans;
4. multiple public-domain Descartes scientific diagrams, including his vortex universe and magnetism;
5. the extremely strong **1711 Index frontispiece** depicting sacred authority and burning books;
6. an additional **1758 engraved Index frontispiece** at nearly 3000×5000 px;
7. a **352-image Wellcome Index** with Public Domain Mark;
8. additional full Agrippa editions at Wellcome, including 612- and 728-image scans with Public Domain Mark;
9. a second 1564 Index scan, useful as backup if the first copy has damaged/marginalized pages.

### GAL-X01 — Galileo autograph/working manuscript of the *Dialogo*, 1632
- **URL:** https://www.loc.gov/item/2021667675/
- **Institution:** Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze, presented via World Digital Library / Library of Congress.
- **Object:** incomplete autograph editing manuscript of *Dialogo sopra i massimi sistemi del mondo*.

Use:
`GAL-X01 autograph manuscript → printed 1632 Dialogo → GAL-X03 sunspot science → Index entry`.

Instead of portrait + text only:
`DES portrait → 1637 Discours diagrams → 1644 vortex universe → magnetism → actual Index wording`.

The vortex and magnetic-field images fit the Noesis visual language exceptionally well while remaining genuine historical scientific material.

Use **ESO-X01/X02** only with an explicit lower-third such as:

This avoids the common documentary mistake of presenting arbitrary occult diagrams as anonymous “forbidden Vatican knowledge.”
