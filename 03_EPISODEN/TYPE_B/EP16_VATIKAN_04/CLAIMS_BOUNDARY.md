# EP16 — Claims Boundary — VERIFIED 2026-08-30

## Safe lead claims
- A strict Roman Index appeared under **Paul IV in 1559**; a Tridentine Index followed under **Pius IV in 1564**.
- Historical Indices distinguished authors/works and also preserved conditional statuses such as `donec corrigatur / donec corrigantur`.
- **Copernicus, Galileo and Descartes must be treated as separate documentary cases**, not as one generic “Church vs science” story.
- The **1948** edition under Pius XII is bibliographically verified at **xxiv + 508 pages**.
- On **14 June 1966**, the CDF stated that the Index retained moral significance but no longer had the force of ecclesiastical law with attached censure.
- On **15 November 1966**, the relevant canonical prohibitions/penalties were declared no longer valid.

## Galileo lock
- On **5 March 1616**, the published Index decree addressed books supporting Copernican propositions; Copernicus and Zúñiga were suspended until corrected. Do not narrate that published decree as if it named Galileo.
- Galileo’s **1632 Dialogo** is a separate later object in the censorship story.
- A checked later historical Index compilation prints the Galileo line with `Decr. 23 Augusti 1634`.
- Therefore: **trial in 1633** and **the decree date printed in the later Index line** are not interchangeable dates. If the page is visible, narration/graphics must match the page.

## Descartes lock
- Several major Descartes writings were indexed in **1663**.
- A checked later Index scan prints on **p.73** for *Meditationes de prima philosophia*: `Donec corrig. Decr. S. Off. 10 oct. 1663`.
- A second Descartes entry on the same page gives `Donec corrig.` with **20 Nov 1663**.
- Safe narration: “major writings / several works were placed on the Index until corrected.”
- Unsafe narration: “all of Descartes was banned on 10 October 1663.”

## Agrippa / esoteric imagery lock
- Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa can be used as a genuine esoteric example because scholarship locates him in the **1559 first class** of prohibited authors.
- Do not imply that the Vatican separately adjudicated each sigil/diagram in *De occulta philosophia*.

## 1948 lock
- Claim safe: final printed Index edition, 1948, **xxiv + 508 pages**.
- Image not yet rights-locked: library reproduction or lawful physical photography is still required.

## Boundaries
- Not all Index entries mean the same thing.
- Do not turn a work-level entry into a total author ban unless the selected edition/category supports that reading.
- Do not equate Index = “ban on all science.”
- Do not show book burning when the evidence is only an Index/decree.
- `donec corrigatur/corrigantur` only appears on screen when the actual selected page supports it.
- Historical artwork being Public Domain does **not** automatically make every modern photograph/reproduction open; use the verified Commons/Met files in the manifest.
- Public accessibility of Vatican web pages does not itself grant an open screenshot/reproduction licence.

## Mystique / tone
The episode does not need invented conspiracy. The tension is stronger when the viewer sees a real portrait, the real book, then the real bureaucratic line that classified it. Let the original pages create the unease.
