# EP04B — NanoBanana / Image Generation Guide V5

**Production principle:** EP04B is an archive-and-motion detective story. Generation fills gaps; it does not replace the evidence.

## Coverage profile

Current planned generated pool:
- **20 MAIN frames**
- **4 RESERVE frames**
- substantially more real historical images, plates, document details and motion layers than EP04A

The number is intentionally smaller. The strongest scenes in EP04B already exist as historical objects. A real Leadbeater plate, a real Serpent Power page or a real Adyar photograph is more valuable than a synthetic substitute.

## Source-object rule

The following are **edit/motion sources, not things to imitate in AI**:
- historical chakra/lotus plates from *The Serpent Power*
- 1924 public-domain *Serpent Power* scan
- Leadbeater 1927 plates
- 1899 Sapta Chakra
- late-18th-century yogin with six chakras
- Leadbeater/Besant portraits and Adyar photographs

## Hard locks

- never generate a fake 1577 manuscript page or fake Sanskrit text;
- never generate a fake 1919 facsimile title page;
- never generate a fake Woodroffe or Atal Bihari Ghose archive portrait;
- the Woodroffe/Ghose collaboration scene uses non-identifying actors/hands and is labeled `REKONSTRUKTION`;
- modern seven-rainbow graphics must look unmistakably modern;
- no `India = six / West = seven` visual shorthand;
- no visual ridicule of Theosophy or clairvoyance;
- no `FAKE`, `DEBUNKED`, red-stamp or conspiracy-board aesthetics;
- motion must preserve visible seams between historical layers rather than making one ancient picture magically morph into the modern map;
- visible editorial typography is built in German during edit; generated historical-looking text is avoided.
