## O01 — Canon 1172
- Institution: Holy See / Vatican
- Verified URL: https://www.vatican.va/archive/cod-iuris-canonici/eng/documents/cic_lib4-cann1166-1190_en.html
- Hero passage: Canon 1172 §1–2 — special and express permission of the local ordinary; priest selected for piety, knowledge, prudence and integrity of life.
- Use: strongest proof that solemn exorcism is institutionally authorised rather than a private priestly initiative.
- Rights: official source, but no open screenshot/reproduction licence found; legal/rights review for finished-film reproduction.

## O02 — 1999 Presentation of the New Rite
- Institution: Holy See, Congregation for Divine Worship and the Discipline of the Sacraments.
- **Primary verified Italian URL:** https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccdds/documents/rc_con_ccdds_doc_1999-01-26_il-rito-degli-esorcismi_it.html
- **Primary verified Spanish URL:** https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccdds/documents/rc_con_ccdds_doc_1999-01-26_il-rito-degli-esorcismi_sp.html
- Hero passages: bishop's permission; distinction from illness/especially psychological illness; speaking/understanding unknown languages; revealing distant/hidden things; strength disproportionate to age/condition; violent aversion to God, Mary, saints, cross and sacred images; approximately ten years of revision work.
- Use: strongest modern evidence reveal.
- Correction: the old `...19990126_exorcisms_en.html` link is not the current verified official document path. Do not retain it as the production source.
