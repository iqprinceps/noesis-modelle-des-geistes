## Reconstructions

- Priest opens ritual book; face not identifiable.
- Episcopal authorisation as abstract document handoff.
- Dark room, table, cross, book — no violence, no horror makeup.
- Macro of a spoken Latin line; **never pass a reconstruction off as a scan**.
- If a ritual diagram is reconstructed, label it as graphic reconstruction and do not imitate a specific manuscript page so closely that provenance becomes ambiguous.

## Exclusions
