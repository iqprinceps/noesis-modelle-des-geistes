## Claim-boundary correction

- The older ritual tradition can be described as warning the exorcist against easy credulity and asking him to distinguish signs from disease.
- The specific modern language about **psychological illness** belongs to the modern rite/Catechism evidence and should not be quoted as if it were verbatim 1614 text.
- Modern dissociation/possession-trance literature documents clinical phenomena and cultural interpretation; it neither proves nor disproves a supernatural ontology.
