## Source-lock rule
For every document that appears legibly in the final cut, production must record:
1. exact archive shelfmark;
2. folio/page if a specific passage is claimed;
3. exact visible text/transcription if highlighted;
4. source/master URL;
5. image-rights grant or open licence;
6. required credit line;
7. crop/alteration restrictions.

A general archive webpage is evidence for institutional facts, but **not** a substitute for the exact document/folio when the narration claims that a visible page says something specific.
