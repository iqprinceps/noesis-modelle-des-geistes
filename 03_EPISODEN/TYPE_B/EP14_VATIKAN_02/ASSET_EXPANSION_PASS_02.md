# EP14 — Asset Expansion Pass 02

**Episode:** Vatican II — *The Archive Beneath Rome*  
**Research pass:** 2026-08-30  
**Purpose:** broaden the visual/evidence pool beyond the first-pass AAV/Commons/Met set, with additional national archives, research libraries, museum collections, facsimile catalogues, IIIF repositories and reuse-safe historical material.

## Rights legend
- **GREEN:** reuse-ready under Public Domain, CC0, CC BY, institutional Open Access or explicit “no known restrictions” statement.
- **AMBER:** correct/relevant object verified, but separate reproduction/editorial permission or licence is required.
- **RED:** discovery/reference only; do not use as a production master without a rights chain.

> This pass deliberately distinguishes **historical evidence** from **later visual reception**. A striking occult or romantic image is never treated as evidence for what a medieval or early-modern document actually says.

# E1 — Henry VIII / Causa Anglica context

## X001 — British Library: England–Rome divorce papers, 1530–1532
- **URL:** https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-001102996
- **Institution:** British Library.
- **Shelfmark:** `Cotton MS Vitellius B XIII`.
- **Verified content:** records and papers concerning England and Rome, 1530–1532, including papers on Henry VIII's divorce from Catherine of Aragon; letters by Richard Croke and material documenting the English campaign for learned/legal support.
- **Rights:** **AMBER** for manuscript photography; catalogue record is reference/evidence only unless BL image rights are cleared.
- **Use:** S1 contextual document montage around the Causa Anglica; strong alternative to repeating the same hero parchment for every sentence.
- **Editorial value:** demonstrates that the 1530 petition existed inside a much larger documentary campaign.

- Do not use Lévi's 1856 Baphomet as medieval Templar evidence.
