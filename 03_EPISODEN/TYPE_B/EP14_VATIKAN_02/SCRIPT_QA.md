## Rights boundary — IMPORTANT
AAV explicitly states that it holds rights over pictures of its documents and requires permission/payment for professional/editorial publication. Therefore:
- AAV web images are discovery/reference until cleared;
- a Commons “Public Domain” tag on a photograph apparently sourced from the Vatican is not enough by itself when provenance conflicts with the AAV rights policy;
- every licensed AAV shot must carry the archive reference and exact credit wording granted by AAV.
