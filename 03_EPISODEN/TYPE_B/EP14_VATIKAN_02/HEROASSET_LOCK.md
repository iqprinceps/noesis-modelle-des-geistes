## HA14-01 — Causa Anglica / letter of 13 July 1530

This document is the episode's non-substitutable visual protagonist.

### Canonical institutional source

Vatican Apostolic Archive — *Exemplaria Praetiosa IV / Causa Anglica*:  
https://www.archivioapostolicovaticano.va/content/aav/en/attivita/ricerca-e-conservazione/progetti/edizioni-di-fonti/exemplaria-praetiosa.html

Verification 2026-08-30: live official Archive page. It identifies the object as the letter from the Lords of England to Clement VII requesting the annulment of Henry VIII's marriage to Catherine of Aragon, dated **13 July 1530**. The Archive gives the physical dimensions as approximately **91.5 × 46 cm**, records **83 signatures** in 13 columns, **81 pending seals in tin skippets**, and four empty skippets.

This source resolves an important production point: narration and graphics should say **83 signatures / 81 seals**, not treat the number of signatories, signatures and surviving seals as interchangeable.
