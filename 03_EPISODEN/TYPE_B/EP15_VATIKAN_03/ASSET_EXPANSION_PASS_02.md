# 12. EDITORIAL EXPANSION SUGGESTIONS

These are small script/picture improvements enabled by the new assets. They do **not** require changing the episode's core structure.

### S1 — make the legend stranger before the document reveal
Current narration mentions Constantine's illness and Sylvester. With X01/X02 available, the first minute can briefly show that the medieval legend was much richer: illness, Peter/Paul dream, miraculous cure, then donation. This raises the mythic atmosphere without inventing anything.

### S3 — distinguish three layers of visual authority
A very strong sequence is now possible:
1. **1246 Sylvester fresco cycle** — medieval legend.
2. **Renaissance Donation engraving/fresco** — monumental papal iconography.
3. **actual manuscript/legal transmission** — words becoming institutional memory.

### S4 — visualise “Valla was not alone” without weakening him
A 3-second Nicholas of Cusa insert (X08/X10) can support the existing claims boundary: Valla was not the first doubter, but his philological assault was unusually systematic and influential.

### S8 — make print transmission a cast of characters
Instead of a generic press montage:
- Valla manuscript →
- **1506 bibliographic lock** →
- **Hutten portrait** →
- **BLB/BSB ca. 1520 debate volume**.
This gives the media transition names, objects and dates.
