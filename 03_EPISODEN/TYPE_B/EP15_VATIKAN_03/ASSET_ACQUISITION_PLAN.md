## AI / reconstruction — only connective tissue

Allowed:
- dark medieval scriptorium with an unidentified copyist;
- macro ink / parchment / scraping texture with **no invented readable Latin**;
- abstract transfer of imperial purple / globe / keys as metaphor;
- printing press movement for S8.

Avoid:
- fake “original Donation” parchment;
- fake Vatican seals presented as evidence;
- occult sigils/alchemy diagrams unrelated to the sources;
- AI portrait labeled as Lorenzo Valla, Sylvester or Constantine;
- invented Latin passages.

## Rights workflow

1. Open the exact source/file page, not a search-result thumbnail.
2. Record creator, institution/object ID, work date, scan/photo author, source URL and exact license separately.
3. For CC BY/CC BY-SA: store exact attribution and license version.
4. For CC0: retain provenance anyway.
5. For PD-Art/PDM: retain source institution and perform final territory/institution check.
6. For BAV/Vatican Museums: obtain written permission where required; do not infer free reuse from age of artwork.
7. Download highest-resolution source only after classification.
8. Preserve original filename and source URL in production manifest.
9. Verify visible text against the exact page before cropping/highlighting.
10. Save a rights screenshot/PDF or rights-page URL alongside the asset record at picture lock.
