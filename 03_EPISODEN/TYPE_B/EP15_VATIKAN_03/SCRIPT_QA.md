## REQUIRED PATCH 1 — Gratian wording

Current S2 says:

> “And eventually this text lands in one of the most influential collections of canon law of the Middle Ages: the Decretum Gratiani. From there it is copied, commented upon and carried onward.”

This is too compressed for a forensic episode. The Donation passages associated with the Gratian tradition are **Paleae/additions** in D.96 c.13–14; scholarship distinguishes them from Gratian’s core compilation.

### Recommended replacement

> “And eventually the text enters the legal tradition surrounding one of the most influential canon-law collections of the Middle Ages: Gratian’s Decretum. In later textual layers, passages of the Donation appear among the additions known as Paleae. The claim is no longer only a story. It now circulates inside an authoritative legal tradition.”

This is more accurate **and** dramatically stronger.
