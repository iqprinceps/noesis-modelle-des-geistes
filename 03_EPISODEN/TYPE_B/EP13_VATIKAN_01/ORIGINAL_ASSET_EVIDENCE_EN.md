# 1. Production-critical primary evidence

## A01 — Vatican: *The Message of Fatima* (2000)

**URL:** https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20000626_message-fatima_en.html  
**Status:** VERIFIED / live / official primary source  
**Rights:** AMBER — evidence/source use is fine; reproduction of Vatican portal text/images outside personal use requires prior written DPC authorisation under the Vatican portal Legal Notes.  
**Production use:** master factual source for S2–S6.

**Exact source locations in the live HTML:**
- Introduction, paragraph beginning **“The first and second parts…”**: first two parts in the Third Memoir (31 Aug 1941), annotations in Fourth Memoir (8 Dec 1941), third part written **3 Jan 1944**, one manuscript.
- Next paragraph: sealed envelope initially with Bishop of Leiria; placed in **Secret Archives of the Holy Office on 4 Apr 1957**.
- Next paragraphs: **John XXIII received it 17 Aug 1959**, returned it without publication; **Paul VI read it 27 Mar 1965**; **John Paul II requested it after the 13 May 1981 assassination attempt**.
- Paragraph beginning **“On 18 July 1981…”**: two envelopes — white with Portuguese original, orange with Italian translation.
- Section **THIRD PART OF THE “SECRET”**: four linked facsimile images + English translation.
- Vision text: angel/flaming sword → bishop dressed in white → ruined city → mountain/cross → bullets and arrows → blood of martyrs.
- Section **CONVERSATION WITH SISTER MARIA LUCIA OF JESUS AND THE IMMACULATE HEART**, 27 Apr 2000: Lucia identifies the letter and handwriting; confirms the principal figure is the Pope but says no specific papal name was given.
- Section **ANNOUNCEMENT MADE BY CARDINAL ANGELO SODANO**: symbolic interpretation; “motherly hand” formulation; crown/bullet provenance statement.

**Editorial note:** this one page can carry the manuscript chronology, archival custody, papal reading chronology, 1981 envelope delivery, the actual vision text and the 2000 interpretation. It should be the source-lock backbone.

# 6. Editorial / claim corrections recommended before English voice lock

## E01 — Keep 17:19

The official Vatican 13 May 1981 audience record and pontificate chronology both support **17:19 / 5:19 p.m.**. This detail is safe and gives the scene documentary precision.

## E02 — Keep the bullet wording broad

Current script language such as **“the bullet that struck him”** / **“one of the projectiles from the attack”** is preferable to a specific claim about where the bullet was recovered.

Reason: official sources conflict:
- current Shrine material: bullet extracted from John Paul II’s body;
- Vatican 2000 Sodano text: bullet remained in the jeep.

Until archival provenance is clarified, do not say “the surgeon removed this exact bullet from his abdomen.”

## E03 — Add the 1984 handover date if useful

The current Shrine source says the projectile was offered to the Shrine on **26 March 1984**, then set into the crown in **1989**. This makes S7 stronger because the object has a traceable chain rather than simply “later”.

Suggested English line:

> “On 26 March 1984, a bullet from the attack was offered to the Shrine. In 1989, it was set into the precious crown.”

Use this only with the provenance caveat in E02.

## E04 — Do not oversell prophecy precision

The Vatican primary document itself says the vision must be interpreted symbolically and does not identify John Paul II by name or specify 13 May 1981. The current script already handles this well. Preserve that restraint — it increases credibility and makes the story stranger, not weaker.

## E05 — Treat the manuscript as the main “scientific” evidence object

This episode has **no scientific claim that needs a scientific diagram**. Do not insert generic brain scans, astronomy or ballistics graphics just because the series sometimes uses scientific imagery.

If a trajectory schematic is desired for S4, make it a clearly labelled **reconstruction diagram**, based only on verified location/event facts. Never present an invented ballistic path as archival evidence.

# 8. Attribution / manifest notes

For every downloaded free asset store:

- canonical file-description URL
- original-file download URL
- creator/photographer
- date
- licence name + licence URL
- screenshot/PDF of the licence page on acquisition date
- original filename
- SHA-256 checksum
- intended shot IDs
- crop/grade notes

For Shrine/Vatican licensed material additionally store the written permission/licence and any required exact credit line.

**Do not rely on a generic “Wikimedia Commons = free” assumption.** D01 is the concrete counterexample: Commons hosts it under a US public-domain theory but explicitly warns it is still copyrighted in Germany and other jurisdictions. Rights must be assessed asset by asset.
