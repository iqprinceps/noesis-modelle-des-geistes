# 10. Quality-control warnings

- A crowd looking upward is evidence of **people looking upward**, not evidence that a supernatural solar event physically occurred.
- Newspaper reporting proves contemporary reporting and public reaction, not the truth of the apparition claim.
- Paleographic/diplomatic analysis can support **document authenticity**, not supernatural content.
- RTP catalogue descriptions are strong acquisition metadata, but exact broadcast frames must be visually checked after licensing/download.
- Modern reposts of old newspaper pages are discovery aids only; always trace them back to Hemeroteca, RTP, Shrine, Vatican, or another authoritative archive.
