https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_20000626_message-fatima_en.html

Verification 2026-08-30: live official Vatican page. The page states that the third part was written on **3 January 1944**, that **there is only one manuscript**, and that it is reproduced photostatically. It documents the transfer to the Holy Office archive in 1957 and the later papal readings.

Commercial exploitation of portal sharing is not permitted.

DPC site-owner contact listed in the Vatican legal notice: `spc@spc.va` (Via della Conciliazione 5, 00120 Vatican City). The legal page should be saved/snapshotted with the eventual permission response.

Request:
- explicit audiovisual reproduction permission for all four manuscript images;
- use in worldwide commercial documentary/YouTube/social distribution;
- crop/zoom/highlight/kinetic locator treatment;
- whether a higher-resolution scan/master can be supplied instead of the legacy web GIFs;
- exact credit/copyright line;
- confirmation of whether the manuscript images are controlled directly by DPC or another Holy See rights office for this use.

**Picture lock:** RED until written authorisation and usable-resolution files are secured.
