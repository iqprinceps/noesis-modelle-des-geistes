The English scripts preserve the factual boundaries of the German source
episodes while rebuilding syntax, hooks, audience questions, transitions and
series handoffs for natural English narration. Eight minutes is a monetization-
aware threshold, not a reason to add filler or slow the voice.

Before voice generation, each episode still requires:

1. episode-level source lock against the cited primary material;
2. native/idiomatic aloud read in the intended narrator register;
3. pronunciation sheet for names, titles and scientific terms;
4. final word-level claim/tone/visual alignment matrix;
5. exact duration from the approved voice master and forced alignment.

The German scripts remain source references. They are not the English voice
masters and must not be sent to the English voice pipeline.
