## Minimum original-asset target before picture lock

The edit should not enter picture lock with only six or seven archive images. Minimum target:

- 5 historical-person portraits;
- 8–10 Blavatsky/document page states;
- 5–7 parietal-eye / pineal scientific visuals;
- 2–3 human-anatomy states;
- 4–6 Ajna/chakra historical images;
- 2–4 Shiva/third-eye museum objects;
- 2–4 Leadbeater source states or original editorial redraws;
- 2–3 Theosophical context images;
- 2–3 DMT paper/data states.

Together with the locked generated-reconstruction prompts, that produces approximately **40–55 distinct usable visual states** before crops and motion design.

## Rights rules

- Public-domain status of an underlying historical work does **not** automatically clear every modern scan, photograph, edition or website layout.
- For EU/Germany delivery, prefer assets explicitly marked CC0, Public Domain, Open Access, or public domain in both source country and the US when applicable.
- Commons hosting is not by itself a legal conclusion; preserve the exact file-page rights metadata.
- Every downloaded binary must record source page, direct download URL, creator/institution, date, license/use basis, retrieval date, SHA-256, dimensions/pages, external path, narration beat and claim limit.
- No generated portrait may be presented as Blavatsky, Spencer, Leydig, Woodroffe, Leadbeater or any historical person.
- Archive/scientific evidence, historical esoteric material and generated reconstruction must remain clearly separated in production metadata.
- Shiva/Ajna imagery supports religious/spiritual history only; it cannot be relabelled as anatomical pineal evidence.
- Reptile parietal-eye material supports vertebrate comparative anatomy only; it cannot be used as proof of a lost human sensory organ.
