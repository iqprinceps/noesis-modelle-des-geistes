## Rights rules

- Public-domain status of the underlying historical work does **not** automatically clear every modern scan, photograph, edition or website layout.
- For EU/Germany delivery, prefer Commons assets explicitly marked CC0 or public domain in source country plus the US, or institution scans with a clear reuse statement.
- Every downloaded binary must record: source page, direct download URL, creator/institution, publication date, license/use basis, retrieval date, SHA-256, dimensions, local/external path and narration beat.
- No generated portrait may be presented as Helena Blavatsky, Leadbeater or a historical scientist.
- Reconstructions of 1888 reading rooms, anatomy labs or meditative experience must be tagged `RECONSTRUCTION` in metadata, never in the viewer-facing image.
