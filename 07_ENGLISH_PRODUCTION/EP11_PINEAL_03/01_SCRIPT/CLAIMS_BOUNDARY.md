# EP11_EN — Claim Map

## Established story anchors

- Helena P. Blavatsky published *The Secret Doctrine* in 1888.
- Volume two contains a section titled “The Races with the Third Eye” beginning on p. 289 and, across the following pages, connects a former third eye, the pineal gland and the Eye of Shiva/Siva within Blavatsky's own occult human history.
- Nineteenth-century comparative anatomy discussed eye-like parietal/pineal structures in vertebrates, including reptiles. This zoological evidence is evidence about those animals, not evidence for Blavatsky's lost-human chronology.
- Older Ajna traditions describe a subtle center and practice associated with the region between the eyebrows; they do not present the modern anatomical formula `Ajna = pineal gland`.
- C. W. Leadbeater's 1927 *The Chakras* summarizes the sixth center with the pituitary body and the seventh with the pineal gland, while later passages in the same work describe a more fluid interaction in which currents from both higher centers may be directed toward the pituitary body.
- Dean et al. (2019) measured cortical DMT in rats with and without an intact pineal gland; pinealectomy did not eliminate the cortical DMT signal, and experimental cardiac arrest increased cortical DMT in both groups.

## Attribution and open meaning

- Blavatsky is presented as a major documented point of synthesis, not necessarily the single inventor of every later version.
- Her occult evolutionary claims remain attributed to her text.
- Leadbeater is evidence of a later esoteric mapping under active negotiation, not evidence of a stable ancient endocrine doctrine.
- The historical origin of an anatomical association does not settle the meaning of a person's spiritual or meditative experience.
- Rat DMT findings do not establish a human pineal-DMT mechanism for dreams, birth, death or mystical experience.

## Production evidence boundary

- Historical claims require readable primary-document pages before any generated reconstruction.
- Generated imagery may visualize atmosphere, subjective experience or conceptual overlap, but must never be presented as archival evidence.
- Any rights-unclear Leadbeater or Woodroffe scan remains research-only until cleared or replaced with an original editorial diagram.
