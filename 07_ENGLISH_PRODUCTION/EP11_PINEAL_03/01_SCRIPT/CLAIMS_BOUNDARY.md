# EP11_EN — Claim Map

## Established story anchors

- Helena P. Blavatsky published *The Secret Doctrine* in 1888.
- Volume two connects a former third eye, the pineal gland and the Eye of Shiva within Blavatsky's own occult human history.
- Nineteenth-century comparative anatomy discussed eye-like parietal/pineal structures in vertebrates.
- Older Ajna traditions describe a subtle center and practice; they do not present the modern anatomical formula `Ajna = pineal gland`.
- C. W. Leadbeater's 1927 *The Chakras* connects the sixth center with the pituitary body and the seventh with the pineal gland.

## Attribution and open meaning

- Blavatsky is presented as a major documented point of synthesis, not necessarily the single inventor of every later version.
- Her occult evolutionary claims remain attributed to her text.
- The historical origin of an anatomical association does not settle the meaning of a person's spiritual or meditative experience.
