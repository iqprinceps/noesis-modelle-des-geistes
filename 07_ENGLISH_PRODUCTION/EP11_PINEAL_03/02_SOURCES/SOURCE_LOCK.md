## Locked Blavatsky document sequence

Use the 1888 Vol. II section as a readable document reveal, not decorative texture:

1. p. 289 — section title “The Races with the ‘Third Eye’”.
2. pp. 294–296 — transition between contemporary natural-history discussion and occult interpretation.
3. pp. 299–301 — strongest pineal/third-eye synthesis and “gland” turn.
4. Optional surrounding pages through p. 306 only when needed to keep the claim in context.

The edit must never imply that zoological evidence on those pages validates Blavatsky's occult human chronology.

## Ajna source boundary

Woodroffe's *The Serpent Power* is useful because it gives a historical English-language presentation of an older Sanskrit subtle-body text. It describes Ajna as a two-petalled lotus between the eyebrows, with mantra and subtle-body symbolism. It does not supply the modern equation `Ajna = pineal gland`.
