## Required media QA

### A. Tuatara / parietal eye
- Acquire the public-domain TimVickers tuatara set first; keep several distinct photographs rather than one repeatedly reframed image.
- Do not caption an ordinary head photograph as a visible functional “third eye” unless the chosen source specifically establishes that feature.
- Acquire Dendy 1899 Plates XI–XIII and preserve full plate/page context.
- Acquire both Spencer 1886 Anolis and Varanus parietal-eye plates.
- Acquire Tilney & Warren 1919 and select several non-duplicative comparative plates.
- Acquire Romero/de Souza 2025 for modern open-access source identity and optional CC BY 4.0 figure use.

### B. Human anatomy, retina and histology
- Acquire Gray plate 719 in original high resolution.
- Acquire CRUK pineal-location vector and preserve CC BY-SA attribution.
- Acquire Cajal's 1911 retina drawing as historical retinal architecture.
- Acquire real human pineal histology at low and very-high magnification.
- Keep any SEM asset on `HOLD_LICENSE_REVIEW` until its publication/license mismatch is resolved.

### C. Circadian pathway
- Acquire Blume/Garbazza/Spitschan SCN diagram and the light–SCN–pineal/melatonin circuit under their exact CC BY attribution.
- Cross-check the visualized route against Moore 1996 and Endotext; animation may simplify appearance, not anatomy.
- Use dense diagrams as guided close-ups/motion sources rather than static full-screen lecture cards.

### D. Chang et al. experiment
- Acquire the complete article/PDF for editorial reference.
- Do not blindly reuse paper figure artwork. If figure rights are not explicitly compatible with production, recreate the graphic from reported values with a citation.
- Lock these study facts in the data graphic notes: 12 healthy adults, randomized crossover, five consecutive evenings per condition, approximately four hours reading before bedtime; reported melatonin suppression 55.12 ± 20.12%, DLMO delayed by more than 1.5 h, sleep latency roughly 10 min longer, lower next-morning alertness.
- Do not convert this study into a universal claim that every phone produces the same magnitude of effect.

### E. Researcher portraits
- Arthur Dendy Royal Society portrait: explicit Royal Society copyright -> permission required.
- Anne-Marie Chang / Charles Czeisler / Jeanne Duffy / Manuel Spitschan / Christine Blume institutional portraits: no reusable license assumed -> permission required.
- If permission is not obtained, omit the portrait. Show the author through paper title/byline and licensed figures. Do not generate a fake historical/research portrait.

### F. Descartes / Elisabeth bridge
- Acquire the museum-quality public-domain/CC0 Descartes portrait.
- Acquire the Wellcome Cartesian nervous-system diagram under CC BY 4.0; it is especially valuable because it visually connects the eye, brain and pineal doctrine.
- Acquire the public-domain Elisabeth portrait.
- For Elisabeth's letter, obtain a stable archival facsimile from Gallica, Internet Archive or another archival host where possible; keep Wikisource only as locator/transcription aid.

### G. Esoteric / religious-art pool
- Acquire Woodroffe's 1918 brow/Ajna diagram and Leadbeater's 1927 chakra/pineal illustrations as **historical esoteric publications**, never as physiology.
- Acquire at least four distinct Met Open Access third-eye works, including one close sculptural face and one narrative print such as *Madan-Bhasma*.
- Preserve object title, culture, date and museum source in metadata even when the image is public domain.
- Contextualize these assets late in the episode as cultural resonance after the biological mechanism is established.
