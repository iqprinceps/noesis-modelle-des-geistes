# EP09_EN — Claim Map

## Established story anchors

- Tuatara and many lizards possess a parietal or “third” eye derived from the pineal complex; the organ includes a simplified retina with photoreceptor cells.
- Several non-mammalian vertebrates possess directly photosensory pineal/parietal systems, while the mammalian pineal is a non-sensory neuroendocrine organ controlled by environmental light indirectly.
- Retinal photoreceptors and pinealocytes show deep evolutionary, molecular and developmental relationships.
- The human pineal gland does not receive environmental light directly.
- Human light timing reaches the pineal through retina → circadian clock → hypothalamic/autonomic relays → superior cervical ganglion → pineal.
- Melatonin functions as a biological darkness/night timing signal; circulating levels normally rise during biological night.
- In Chang et al. (2015), twelve healthy adults completed randomized crossover conditions involving about four hours of reading before bed for five consecutive evenings. Under those conditions, the light-emitting e-reader condition suppressed evening melatonin, delayed circadian timing, increased sleep latency and reduced next-morning alertness relative to a printed book.
- Descartes assigned the pineal gland a central role in his mind-body physiology and called it the principal seat of the soul; Princess Elisabeth challenged the intelligibility of immaterial soul-body causation in 1643 correspondence.

## Attribution and open meaning

- Species-specific behavioral functions of the parietal eye remain tied to zoological sources. The script now describes it conservatively as a simple photoreceptive organ rather than assigning the same detailed timing functions to every cited species.
- Evolution is described as a branching/shared relationship, never as the simple claim that a reptile eye became the human pineal gland.
- The Chang et al. effect sizes remain bound to that experiment’s exposure duration, brightness, device and participant sample. They are not universal phone/screen constants.
- `Third eye` at the end is a viewer interpretation question, not a claim of hidden human vision.

## Do not claim

- The human pineal gland is literally a vestigial third eye with a hidden lens or retina.
- Pineal activity produces paranormal perception, visions or spiritual sight.
- Melatonin itself “knocks the body unconscious”.
- Any evening screen necessarily produces the same melatonin suppression or phase delay measured by Chang et al.
- A generic living-tuatarа photograph visibly proves the functional parietal eye unless the specific image/source establishes it.
