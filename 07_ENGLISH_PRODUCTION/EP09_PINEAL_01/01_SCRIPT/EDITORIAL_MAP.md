# EP09_EN — Editorial Map

## Viewer promise

Nature really built a third eye. The episode reveals what happened when the
ancient relationship between light and biological time changed form in humans.

## Story movements

1. Living third-eye reveal: tuatara and parietal eye.
2. Light as timing information rather than image.
3. Evolutionary relationship among retinal and pineal tissues.
4. Human detour: retina to circadian clock to pineal gland.
5. Payoff: `The sky becomes a molecule.`
6. Personal relevance: illuminated-reader experiment and biological night.
7. Meaning turn: the real biology already carries the strangeness.
8. Descartes and Elisabeth open EP10.

## Interaction

The final question asks whether `third eye` better describes direct light
sensing or the hidden conversion of darkness into inner time. It follows the
payoff and naturally points toward the next episode.

## Primary visual anchors for later production

- authentic tuatara/parietal-eye material;
- retinal and pineal comparative anatomy;
- human retina/circadian pathway explained as motion rather than a dashboard;
- Chang et al. evening-reader study and apparatus/context;
- distinct human night imagery, faces and lived evening behavior;
- Descartes identity anchor and a new Pineal-anatomy state for the handoff.
