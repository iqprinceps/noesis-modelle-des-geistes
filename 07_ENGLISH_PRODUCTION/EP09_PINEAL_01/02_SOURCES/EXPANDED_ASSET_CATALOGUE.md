# EP09_EN — Expanded Asset Catalogue

Reviewed: 2026-08-30
Scope: first English pineal episode, `EP09_PINEAL_01 — The Eye Within`

This catalogue is intentionally larger than the final edit. Its purpose is to prevent the picture edit from stalling because one rights issue, one unusable crop or one weak source image forces a second research pass.

## Coverage summary

The production pool now includes **56 researched asset records** across these groups:

- living tuatara photography and close-ups;
- historical parietal-eye and Sphenodon anatomy;
- modern open-access pineal evolution science;
- human pineal location and real histology;
- retina / SCN / circadian / melatonin pathway diagrams;
- the Chang–Aeschbach–Duffy–Czeisler e-reader experiment;
- researcher portraits and identity sources;
- Descartes portraits and original nervous-system / brain diagrams;
- Princess Elisabeth portrait and primary-document pathway;
- Ajna / chakra diagrams from historical esoteric publications;
- museum-grade Shiva / third-eye iconography from The Met Open Access.

## Recommended visual ratio

For an approximately eight-minute edit, do **not** turn the episode into a slideshow of diagrams. A good target is:

- 20–25% living animal / naturalistic imagery;
- 20–25% archival anatomy and primary-source plates;
- 20–25% human retina / circadian / pineal mechanism;
- 10–15% modern human evening / study reconstruction;
- 10–15% historical faces and documents;
- 5–10% cultural/esoteric imagery, clearly contextualized.

The esoteric material works best as a late visual resonance after the biological mechanism has been established, not as the evidentiary basis of the opening claim.
