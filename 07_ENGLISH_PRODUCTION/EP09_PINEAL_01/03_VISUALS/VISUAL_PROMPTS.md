# EP09_EN — Visual Production Prompts

Status: production-ready prompt plan; archive/science evidence must remain visually distinguishable from generated reconstruction.
Format default: 16:9, cinematic documentary realism, restrained contrast, natural texture, no embedded text, no fake archival marks, no logos.

## VIS-01 — Living third eye / tuatara opener
Reference assets: EP09-A03 living tuatara photo + EP09-A02 historical anatomy plates.
Prompt: Extreme macro documentary portrait of a living tuatara in cool natural daylight, head angled slightly upward so the crown of the skull is visually important, highly detailed scales, calm alert eye, shallow depth of field, real zoological photography language, no fantasy glow, no mystical symbols, no exaggerated third eyeball. The image should create the unsettling impression that something anatomically important exists above the ordinary eyes without inventing visible anatomy.
Use: first 15–20 seconds, intercut with authentic source material.

## VIS-02 — Parietal eye anatomical reconstruction
Reference assets: EP09-A01, EP09-A02, Romero 2025 anatomy description.
Prompt: Scientifically grounded cutaway reconstruction of a reptilian parietal eye on the dorsal skull, simplified lens-like structure and photoreceptive retinal layer shown in anatomical section, clean naturalistic tissue rendering, subtle daylight entering from above, museum-quality scientific visualization, no fantasy iris, no supernatural beam, no labels in-image.
Use: after authentic plate reveal; clearly mark in edit as reconstruction.

## VIS-03 — Light as biological time
Reference: non-mammalian pineal complex claim sources C01–C03.
Prompt: A restrained cinematic time-lapse concept: reptile silhouette on sun-warmed rock from late afternoon into twilight, sky brightness changing while the animal remains nearly still, body and environment changing with the light cycle, visual emphasis on duration and rhythm rather than action, realistic ecology, no magical effects.
Use: bridge from anatomy into timing.

## VIS-04 — Retina to pineal pathway
Reference assets: EP09-A04 and EP09-A05; sources C04–C05.
Prompt: High-end medical visualization of a human head in transparent sagittal cutaway, retina receiving cool morning light, a subtle luminous signal path traveling to the suprachiasmatic nucleus and through the autonomic relay toward the pineal gland, anatomically plausible proportions, sparse visual language, no dashboard UI, no neon cyberpunk, no text labels inside the frame.
Use: animated mechanism sequence.

## VIS-05 — The sky becomes a molecule
Reference: C04–C06.
Prompt: Poetic scientific transition from a real sunset through a human eye into a close macro visualization of blood chemistry at night, cinematic but biologically restrained, the environment fading from warm daylight into darkness while a small pineal gland becomes the final anatomical destination, visual metaphor for external light becoming an internal timing signal, no literal molecule shaped like a sky, no psychedelic imagery.
Use: centerpiece/payoff around “The sky becomes a molecule.”

## VIS-06 — Evening screen experiment
Reference: EP09-A06; Chang et al. 2015.
Prompt: Controlled sleep-laboratory reconstruction, one adult participant reading a light-emitting e-reader in a dim clinical room during late evening, simple bed and monitoring context, no identifiable brand, realistic low-light exposure, documentary research atmosphere, no dramatic blue glow, no horror styling. A parallel shot should show the same participant reading a printed book under matched study conditions.
Use: study setup reconstruction after authentic paper/document shot.

## VIS-07 — Circadian delay data visualization
Reference: Chang et al. 2015 values.
Prompt: Minimal editorial data visualization on a dark neutral background showing two clean trajectories for printed-book versus light-emitting-reader conditions: later melatonin timing, suppressed evening melatonin and slightly longer sleep latency. No copied figure design, no journal branding, no decorative 3D chart, no invented numbers. Build from the cited numeric results in the production manifest.
Use: motion graphic generated by editor/Codex, not image generation if exact numbers are required.

## VIS-08 — Biological night in ordinary life
Prompt: Quiet modern apartment at midnight, person sitting near a window after setting down a glowing device, room mostly dark with realistic practical light, subtle fatigue and introspection, city lights outside, cinematic documentary tone, no melodrama, no visible clock text required.
Use: personalize the mechanism after study evidence.

## VIS-09 — Human pineal / Descartes threshold
Reference assets: EP09-A04 + EP09-A07.
Prompt: Match-cut concept: anatomical pineal gland in a historical medical plate transitions into a dim seventeenth-century study with parchment, anatomical sketches and a human silhouette resembling period European portraiture but not a generated likeness of Descartes. The final frame should hand off to the authentic public-domain Descartes portrait.
Use: transition to EP10.

## VIS-10 — Elisabeth letter cliffhanger
Reference asset: EP09-A09.
Prompt: Close cinematic view of a seventeenth-century letter on a wooden writing desk, ink and paper texture accurate to the period, female hand just leaving the page, candlelight with readable-document framing reserved for an authentic facsimile overlay in edit. Do not generate fake French wording; leave the paper text indistinct so the real archival scan can replace it.
Use: final cliffhanger into EP10.

## Editorial rules

- Every generated scientific reconstruction must be separated in the edit from documentary evidence by sequence logic, captioning or source reveal.
- Never generate a fake historical document and present it as authentic.
- Never generate a visible “third eye” on a human forehead.
- Avoid repeated dark-blue neuroscience imagery; vary living animal, paper/archive, daylight ecology, anatomy, laboratory, ordinary night and historical portraiture.
- Exact charts and document quotations should be composited from verified source data/assets rather than image-generation text.
