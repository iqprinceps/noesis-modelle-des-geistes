# Viewer Analysis — German EP07 Material

## Strongest cold hook

The most arresting fact is not simply that a Salem witness described a pattern
that resembles sleep paralysis. It is the human conversion of an invisible
night into institutional power:

> A man wakes in a locked room, cannot speak or move, and feels a woman on his
> chest. Years later he names a real woman under oath. Eight days after that
> testimony, she is hanged.

This opens with sensation, introduces a named source, and lands a consequence
within the first half minute. The first original document must appear before the
viewer has time to classify the opening as generic horror.

## Question that can hold the whole film

**When does an invisible experience become public truth—and can that truth enter
the next person's body?**

The first half follows private sensation becoming art, folklore, ritual and
court evidence. The second half reverses direction: experience can precede a
story, but a known story may then intensify fear, expectation and the remembered
shape of later episodes.

## Forward-driving loops

1. What happened in Coman's room—and why did Salem accept his explanation?
2. If Salem did not invent the pattern, why does chest pressure recur under so
   many unrelated names?
3. Did stories create the encounter, or did recurring encounters create the
   stories?
4. Hufford reversal: what if the pattern can arrive before the name?
5. Egypt/Denmark reversal: if culture need not create the first episode, can it
   still change the next one?
6. Final synthesis: body gives raw material; culture gives it a face; the face
   returns as expectation.

## Retention diagnosis of the German structure

The German EP07 has unusually strong bones: Salem consequence, Fuseli visual
recognition, Hufford inversion, then the Egypt/Denmark counterturn. Its risks are
the long folklore-name run, repeated returns to the same Salem/Fuseli states,
and a late Art Bell handoff that can feel like an advertisement for EP08 rather
than the completion of EP07.

The English edit therefore:

- completes the Coman/Bishop consequence in the cold open;
- compresses folklore into three embodied rooms instead of an encyclopedia;
- treats ritual responses as human logic under a different causal model;
- makes Hufford a genuine reversal, not a biography block;
- uses the cross-cultural study as a second reversal and states its sampling
  limits inside the story rather than in a disclaimer card;
- resolves the episode on the body–story feedback loop before a short optional
  media-speed coda;
- uses one low-friction interaction choice, `EXPERIENCE / STORY`, after the
  viewer understands both sides.

## Emotional and visual modulation

The eye moves through: intimate warm Salem room → bright parchment evidence →
public courtroom faces → saturated canonical art → geographically varied human
interiors and art objects → daylight field research → matched contemporary
interview spaces → clean scientific source → tactile feedback abstraction →
empty public record room. Darkness is punctuation, not the default exposure.
