## Editorial promise

In 1692 Richard Coman gave sworn testimony that Bridget Bishop entered his
locked room, lay on his body and left him unable to speak or move. The film asks
how a private, invisible experience becomes public evidence—and whether the
story a culture gives that experience can alter the next episode itself.
