## Date and wording checks

- Coman's described event was about eight years before his 1692 testimony. The
  script never says the night itself occurred in 1692.
- Coman testified 2 June 1692; Bishop was hanged 10 June 1692.
- Bishop's prosecution contained many accusations and indictments. The script
  explicitly refuses a single-testimony causal claim.
- The English script uses `lay across his chest` as a natural rendering of the
  deposition's `lay upon my Brest or body` and retains attribution to Coman.
