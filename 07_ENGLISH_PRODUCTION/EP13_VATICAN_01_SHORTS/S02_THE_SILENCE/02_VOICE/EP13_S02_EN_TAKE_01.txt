In 1957, a sealed envelope was carried to Rome and locked in a Vatican archive.

The woman who wrote it was alive.

She could have been asked what was in it, on any day, for the next forty-eight years.

Nobody asked.

Two popes opened that envelope. Both read it. Both closed it again and said nothing at all.

Everyone else was told only that it existed.

So people filled the silence themselves.

With a war. With the end of the world. With the deaths of popes.

Every one of those ideas grew out of a single fact.

The page stayed closed.

Then a man was shot in Saint Peter's Square, survived, and asked for it from a hospital bed.

What would you have assumed was inside?

Answer before you look it up.
