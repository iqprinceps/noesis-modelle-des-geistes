Inside a crown in Portugal, among the gold and the pearls, there is a bullet.

It was fired at a pope in 1981.

He survived.

Two months later, still recovering from surgery, he asked for an envelope.

It had been sealed for thirty-seven years.

Two popes before him had opened that envelope, read what was inside, and closed it again without telling anyone.

The text was written in 1944 by a woman who was still alive, and who could have been asked at any time.

Nobody asked.

He read it from a hospital bed, two months after somebody shot him.
