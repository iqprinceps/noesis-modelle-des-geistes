# EP04A_EN Visual Retention Plan

## Planning reference — not a quota

- shot count follows the aligned voice and meaningful visual changes;
- no minimum distinct-visual count;
- no still, document render, card, or clip state returns after leaving the timeline;
- 10 candidate motion concepts, including 5 abstract sequences; consolidate or omit after alignment

The German V3 is the visual benchmark. The English version keeps its archive
strength while replacing language-bound assets and making the distinction
between archive and reconstruction more visible.
