## Selection rule

Research catalogues remain broad pools. They are **not** shot lists. Before acquisition, each episode gets a 25–40-state cue list. A new crop of the same source does not count as a new visual state.

Every derived crop/extract must carry `derivative_of=<canonical asset_id>` in the production cue list or acquisition manifest.
