## Title

Researchers Recorded Six Sleep Paralysis Episodes

## Description

Researchers interrupted sleep in 16 healthy participants—and recorded six isolated sleep-paralysis episodes across four experimental nights.

The 1992 protocol did not produce an episode after every interruption or in every participant. But when episodes occurred, participants knew they were in the lab and could not move. The recordings captured waking awareness while muscle atonia persisted.
