## Title

The Old Hag Came Before the Name | Sleep Paralysis

## Description

A door opens. Footsteps cross the room. The mattress sinks—and you cannot move.

David Hufford described experiencing this sequence before he became a folklorist and before, he said, he knew Newfoundland's name for the visitor: the Old Hag. REM physiology can explain the paralysis. Hufford's harder question is why footsteps, weight and intention recur in other lives.

Watch the full investigation: https://youtu.be/dXwqAWc74Ts

Which came first: the legend—or the encounter?
