# EP05 EN Shorts — Editorial and claim lock

## Shared boundaries

- Physiology is stated positively: REM-related muscular atonia can persist into waking awareness and account for immobility.
- The felt presence, footsteps, weight and intention remain reported experience and open interpretation.
- No wording asserts an external being, dismisses the encounter, or uses defensive `not proof` framing.
- Named people and experiments receive authentic identity/source anchors before reconstruction is asked to carry identity.
- Generated people are anonymous dramatizations and are never presented as David Hufford, Takeuchi, a named participant, or original footage.

## S01

Hufford reported a structured encounter before he said he knew the Newfoundland
Old Hag tradition. The ending asks whether legend shapes encounter, recurring
encounter shapes legend, or both participate in a feedback loop.

## S02

Takeuchi et al. (1992) used a nocturnal sleep-interruption schedule with sixteen
healthy subjects over four experimental nights. The abstract reports six ISP
episodes, awareness of lying in the laboratory with inability to move, all but
one reporting auditory/visual hallucinations and unpleasant emotions, and PSG
evidence of a REM/wake dissociated state with persistent muscle atonia.
