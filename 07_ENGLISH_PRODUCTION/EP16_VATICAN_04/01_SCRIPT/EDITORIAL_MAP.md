## Viewer promise

Three years after the Second World War, Rome printed 508 pages of forbidden
books. Eighteen years later the system ended with a single administrative
notice. The episode follows what happens to a book when an institution reads it,
judges it, and turns it into a line.

## Structural change against the German master

Three defects, three fixes:

1. **1966 was given away in the cold open** and then structured as the payoff.
   The opening now states that the system ended abruptly, by paper rather than
   revolution, and withholds the date and the content until the closing act.
2. **The episode had no human being in it.** Galileo and Descartes appear only
   as books by design, which left nobody to care about. Two second-person beats
   supply the missing person: the scholar applying for permission to read, and
   the censor choosing between prohibition and correction.
3. **A production note had reached the voice track.** The German S7 explained the
   1948 scan rights problem to the audience. Removed. Reconstruction is handled
   by the visual grammar in the rights lock, never narrated.

## Story movements

1. 1948, 508 pages, Galileo and Descartes present. The abrupt ending promised, undated.
2. What the object is: ink, author, title, note. Donec corrigatur.
3. Why lists became necessary: printing speed, then 1559 and 1564.
4. A book becomes a line: the Dialogo, and the entry.
5. Descartes 1663 and conditional prohibition as editing rather than destruction.
6. The reader applying for permission. Then the censor's choice. Viewer decision.
7. The chain: print, sell, read, judge, correct, restrict. Prohibition as attention.
8. Return to 1948 as a physical object and a record of its own history.
9. 14 June 1966. Moral force retained, legal force with censures removed.
10. The list survives as a counter-archive of reading.
11. Series form named, then the Lancashire farmhouse handoff.

## Interaction

Placed at movement 6: `BAN or CORRECT`. It is the actual historical instrument
pair, so the viewer is not giving an opinion about censorship, they are operating
it. Repeated in the final line.

## Primary visual anchors

- 1948 volume as a closed bibliographic object; page texture supplied only by
  older dated public-domain editions, never captioned as 1948;
- printing process, hands, type, binding, bookselling, reading;
- entries as typography: the moment a name becomes a line;
- the 1966 notification as a physically small document beside the volume;
- deliberate absence of flames, chains and dungeon imagery.
