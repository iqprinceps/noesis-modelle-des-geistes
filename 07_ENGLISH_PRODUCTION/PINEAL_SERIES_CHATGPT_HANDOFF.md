# Pineal Series — ChatGPT review and research handoff

## What already exists

The four English scripts in `EP09_PINEAL_01` through `EP12_PINEAL_04` are
independent retention adaptations of the approved German series. They are not
literal translations and must not be replaced with a generic educational
rewrite.

Each episode already contains:

- a canonical English voice script;
- an editorial/retention map;
- a claim-boundary map;
- a script QA note with a realistic narration estimate.

The next useful step is source lock, native-English aloud review and original
asset research. Production, voice generation and paid visual generation have
not been authorized by this handoff.

## Paste this instruction into ChatGPT

Review the NOESIS pineal series in the connected GitHub repository. Read the
German scripts under `03_EPISODEN/TYPE_B/EP09_ZIRBELDRUESE_01` through
`EP12_ZIRBELDRUESE_04`, then read the English packages under
`07_ENGLISH_PRODUCTION/EP09_PINEAL_01` through `EP12_PINEAL_04`, including the
global retention and script QA documents.

Treat the existing English scripts as strong working masters. Do not translate
the German text again and do not normalize all four episodes into one formula.
Evaluate each episode from the viewer's perspective for curiosity, retention,
visual potential, emotional progression, interaction and series continuation.
Preserve the distinct engines: living biological wonder in EP09, the human
Descartes–Elisabeth conflict in EP10, a document-led occult genealogy in EP11,
and an experimental threshold mystery in EP12. Keep the tone cinematic,
mysterious and intellectually honest rather than lesson-like. Do not add
generic disclaimers, debunking language, repetitive summaries or filler added
only to cross eight minutes. Do not weaken a claim immediately after presenting
it; place necessary evidence boundaries at the moment where they open the next
question.

For each episode, return:

1. a short viewer-retention verdict;
2. only the exact script changes that materially improve native spoken English,
   tension or clarity;
3. a source-lock table mapping every factual claim to the best available primary
   source or authoritative scientific source, with page/section and URL;
4. an original-asset shortlist containing source URL, creator/institution, date,
   rights/license status, exact story use and the line of narration it supports;
5. a visual progression proposal mixing people, faces, animals, laboratories,
   historical rooms, readable documents, scientific mechanisms, mystical
   reconstructions and abstract threshold imagery;
6. a list of unresolved claims or rights questions that must be checked before
   voice or asset lock.

Document shots must show the complete relevant passage at readable scale and
highlight only the words being discussed. Do not propose full-screen abstracts,
cropped fragments or internal production labels as viewer cards. Avoid repeated
images, empty-room monotony and dark-blue sameness. Generated reconstructions
may be inventive where appropriate, but archive, scientific evidence and
reconstruction must never be confused in the research record.

Do not claim that you edited or pushed repository files. The standard GitHub
connection in ChatGPT is read-only. Return proposed text and research records in
a form that Codex can apply and commit.

## Repository policy for assets

This repository intentionally tracks scripts, prompts, source URLs, rights and
provenance records, manifests, timelines, subtitles, metadata and pipeline code.
Large images, audio, PDFs, generated clips and rendered videos are ignored and
belong in the external production storage. ChatGPT may locate assets and prepare
the acquisition/provenance manifest, but the actual media should be downloaded,
verified and integrated by the production pipeline or Codex rather than pushed
through the standard ChatGPT GitHub connection.

Small textual research files can be applied to GitHub afterward. A separate,
deliberate Git LFS decision would be required before placing binary production
assets in version control.
