# EP03 PEAR — Preproduction Visual Lock

## Viewer diagnosis of the German material

The episode has excellent human material and the most inherently interactive
question. The current cards, however, turn the mystery into a statistics lecture.
`200 tosses`, Operator 10's four statistics, and replication results arrive as
complete infographic slides. The palette is coherent, but the format feels
corporate and allows the viewer to consume the conclusion without experiencing it.

Operator 10 must be a reveal, not a dashboard.

## Keep / rework / retire

### KEEP

- authentic Jahn, Dunne, McDonnell, Princeton, laboratory, and machine archive;
- strong human-scale images of the random-event generator and mechanical cascade;
- language-neutral machine details and real publication/document evidence;
- the underlying two-result contrast: decades of effect versus failed replication.

### REWORK

- all statistics become sequential physical or motion states;
- the cumulative deviation is drawn live against zero;
- Operator 10 appears first as an anonymous block inside fourteen million trials;
- replication is shown as three synchronized laboratories whose expected line never appears.

### RETIRE

- `PE_CARD_MASSE` in its four-block form;
- `PE_CARD_OPERATOR` in its four-statistic form;
- multi-sentence result cards and corporate bar-chart panels;
- generic glowing-brain imagery or superhero-style telekinesis beams;
- any literal force ray moving balls or bits.

## Visual story

Start with a respected engineer confronting an invisible deviation. Make the
viewer search the line. Then reveal that half of what they have been searching for
leans on one unnamed person. The visual grammar moves from machine to dataset to
human identity gap.

## Abstract / mystical layer

PEAR's supernatural possibility should feel mathematical: an invisible field
barely deforms noise, intention arrives on the wrong side of a clock, fourteen
million points organize around a human-shaped absence, and a control signal becomes
too orderly. The images should make agency feel present without showing a visible
force causing a result.

## First 60-second lock

- 0–7 s: Jahn in the basement with a physical machine; one bit changes unnoticed.
- 7–14 s: ten thousand marks fill the frame; only one departs from expectation.
- 14–20 s: `28 years` appears over real lab time passing.
- 20–28 s: promise that one unnamed operator later accounts for about half the excess.
- 28–40 s: NASA/Princeton/Jahn identity established with authentic archive.
- 40–50 s: McDonnell aircraft and the pilot-interference fear.
- 50–60 s: basement door, PEAR name, machine powers on.
