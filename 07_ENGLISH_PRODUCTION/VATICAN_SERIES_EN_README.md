## Design

`VATICAN_SERIES_RETENTION_ARCHITECTURE.md` carries the series design, the four
defects it corrects against the German drafts, the withheld reveal and viewer
decision per episode, and the open production risks.

Each episode has an editorial map in `01_SCRIPT/EDITORIAL_MAP.md` recording its
viewer promise, story movements, structural changes against the German master,
interaction placement, visual anchors and its own source-lock gate.

## Where the facts live

Facts, claim boundaries, source registers, asset research and rights locks stay
in the German packages and remain the authority:

```
03_EPISODEN/TYPE_B/EP13_VATIKAN_01/
03_EPISODEN/TYPE_B/EP14_VATIKAN_02/
03_EPISODEN/TYPE_B/EP15_VATIKAN_03/
03_EPISODEN/TYPE_B/EP16_VATIKAN_04/
03_EPISODEN/TYPE_B/EP17_VATIKAN_05/
```

Nothing in the English scripts introduces a claim outside those boundaries. Where
a script narrates something the German package had only referenced, the episode's
editorial map records it as a source-lock gate.

## Before voice generation

1. episode-level source lock against the cited primary material, following each
   editorial map's own list;
2. EP17 case detail verification, treated as a hard gate;
3. native aloud read in the intended narrator register;
4. pronunciation sheet per episode;
5. hero-asset rights resolution for EP14 and EP16, or confirmed reconstruction path;
6. claim, tone and visual alignment matrix;
7. exact duration from the approved voice master and forced alignment.
