# EP12_EN — Claim Map

## Established story anchors

- DMT is endogenous and mammalian biology contains a biosynthetic pathway involving INMT.
- Dean et al. 2019 reported extracellular DMT in rat visual cortex with intact pineal glands and after pinealectomy.
- The study reported an increase in measured cortical DMT after experimentally induced cardiac arrest in rats.
- The reported measurement and increase did not require the pineal gland under those experimental conditions.
- The study used cortical microdialysis and downstream chemical analysis; absence of the melatonin signal helped verify pineal removal.
- Human studies have examined phenomenological similarities between DMT and near-death experiences.

## Attribution and open meaning

- Strassman's proposed pineal roles around birth or death remain attributed speculation.
- Every experimental statement keeps species, brain region, method and condition visible.
- Human near-death chemistry, local concentration, biological function and any causal bridge to experience remain open research questions.
