# EP12_EN — Editorial Map

## Viewer promise

A measured DMT signal changes after cardiac arrest in rats even when the pineal
gland has been removed. The episode follows what this experiment establishes
and the larger human question it opens.

## Story movements

1. Final-moment claim and 2019 contradiction in the cold open.
2. Endogenous DMT and the importance of place, concentration and time.
3. Why culture placed DMT inside the pineal gland.
4. Pinealectomy reveal: cortical DMT remains detectable.
5. Microdialysis makes the invisible experiment understandable.
6. Cardiac arrest and the measured cortical rise.
7. Viewer thought experiment: what would persuasive human measurement require?
8. Human near-death phenomenology and the unmapped causal bridge.
9. Series payoff: organ → network, point → process, gate → screen.

## Interaction

The final question asks whether the measured result makes the last vision less
or more mysterious. It follows the evidence and invites interpretation without
making the interpretation a scientific claim.

## Primary visual anchors for later production

- Dean et al. 2019 paper, authorship, method and result context;
- clear visual model of intact versus pinealectomized animals;
- microdialysis and chromatographic comparison shown as a procedure;
- one legible result reveal rather than a wall of charts;
- Strassman identity/book context for attributed speculation;
- human faces and distinct subjective threshold imagery;
- no surgical spectacle and no generic neon molecule loop.
