## Script-change recommendations

1. Keep the cold open structure. The contradiction is scientifically supported and is the episode's best retention engine.
2. Replace broad uses of “cortical DMT” with “DMT measured in the rat visual cortex” on first mention in each major section. Later shorthand can remain “cortical DMT” after the location is established.
3. Prefer “the measured DMT signal increased after experimentally induced cardiac arrest” to wording that could imply every rat showed the same magnitude of increase.
4. Keep the sentence that a human death-time DMT trajectory has not been directly measured. It is an essential evidence boundary and also opens the next mystery.
5. Keep DMT/NDE overlap explicitly phenomenological. Never say the study shows DMT causes NDEs.
6. Strassman should appear as an attributed source of the modern pineal-DMT hypothesis, not as evidence for a pineal death release.
