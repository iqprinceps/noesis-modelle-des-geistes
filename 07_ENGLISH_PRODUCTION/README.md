# NOESIS English — canonical production root

This directory contains the canonical English adaptations. The writing and
retention brief is
[`../02_GUIDES/CHATGPT_DREHBUCH_BRIEF.md`](../02_GUIDES/CHATGPT_DREHBUCH_BRIEF.md);
English language and localization decisions follow
[`00_GLOBAL/ENGLISH_PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md`](00_GLOBAL/ENGLISH_PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md).

An English episode is an adaptation, not a literal translation. Facts and
source identity remain accurate; hook, syntax, dramatic order, interaction and
visual language are rebuilt for an English-speaking audience.

## Episode contract

```text
EPxx_NAME/
  README.md
  episode.json
  01_SCRIPT/       canonical narration, editorial map, claims and script QA
  02_VOICE/        manifests, pronunciation and alignment metadata
  03_VISUALS/      retention plan, source register, prompts and visual metadata
  04_AUDIO/        music/SFX plan and text manifests
  05_TIMELINE/     aligned cue sheets and timeline data
  06_RENDER/       text manifests only; binary renders remain local
  07_THUMBNAIL/    concepts and metadata; image exports remain local
  08_SUBTITLES/    SRT/VTT and subtitle QA
  09_UPLOAD/       episode-specific SEO, metadata and publication records
```

`01_SCRIPT/VOICE_SCRIPT_EN.txt` is the narration authority. A superseded voice
or emergency cut cannot silently replace it.

## Visual and editorial continuity

Narration, people, documents, cards, clips, music and SFX must agree at the
spoken moment. Original assets establish identity and evidence; generated
material supplies missing action, subjective experience and abstract or
mystical visualization. Avoid repeated assets, empty-room monotony, unreadable
documents and permanent production labels.

Series continuity matters, but it does not override episode-specific viewer
judgment. The strongest existing episode is a quality reference, not a template.
