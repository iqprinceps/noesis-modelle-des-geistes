## Viewer promise

A real bullet from the 1981 attempt on John Paul II is set in a crown in
Portugal. The episode traces backwards from that object to a sealed text two
earlier popes read and closed again, and asks what the wounded man was looking
for when he finally opened it.

## Story movements

1. Object, survivor, sealed envelope, two identical dates. Two questions planted.
2. A reported vision becomes a physical object: 1944 manuscript, 1957 to Rome.
3. Two popes read and reseal. Silence becomes a repeated decision.
4. 13 May 1981. The attack, then the hospital request of 18 July. Viewer decision.
5. The 2000 facsimile. The vision as sequence, and what the page never says.
6. Recognition rather than prediction. Sodano, Lucia, the Vatican commentary.
7. The bullet leaves Rome and enters the crown in 1989. Object payoff.
8. Series form named: an envelope gave a vision a body.
9. Handoff: eighty-three signatures, eighty-one seals, and an archive sold by weight.

## Withheld reveal

The content of the vision is **not** stated in the cold open. Movement 1 may name
the envelope, the readers and the silence only. The vision text is the movement 5
payoff, and the strongest single line in the episode is that the bishop in white
does not survive while the man reading it in 1981 did. Any hook draft that
describes the vision destroys the midpoint.

## Interaction

Placed at movement 4, immediately before the document reveal, and repeated in the
final line: `WORLD or MYSELF`. It asks the viewer to make the same reading
decision the episode then examines, so the answer is still active during the
payoff.

## Primary visual anchors

- return to Fatima in gold and daylight; crown wide, then in to the projectile.
