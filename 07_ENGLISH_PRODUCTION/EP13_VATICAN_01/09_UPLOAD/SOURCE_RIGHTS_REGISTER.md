## No ShareAlike, and why that mattered

Every still in this film is cropped and moved by the Ken Burns pass, which makes
the film Adapted Material rather than a collection of unmodified works. Under CC
BY-SA that would oblige the whole film to carry a ShareAlike licence, and YouTube
has no way to express one. Eight CC BY-SA stills were found in the finished cut
during the pre-publication audit and all eight were replaced. Two of them also
carried a photographer's watermark burned into the frame, and one, a smiling
cardinal portrait sitting under a reflective line, was wrong for its beat anyway.
The replacements are recorded in `COMMONS_SHAREALIKE_REPLACEMENT_MANIFEST.csv`
with the reason for each.

## Never on screen

`EP13-HA02`, the Vatican's photostatic reproduction of the manuscript, is a claim
and locator source only. The portal terms do not grant the reuse this production
needs, and the reveal does not depend on the image. It appears nowhere in the cut;
the beat is carried by the treatment in `03_VISUALS/REVEAL_ACT_TREATMENT.md`.

## Generated material

71 shots and 7 clips are AI reconstructions produced for this
production with Nano Banana Pro and Veo on Vertex AI, plus
10 typographic cards composited on generated substrates. No likeness of any
real person is synthesised: every named figure in this episode appears only in a
genuine photograph. The upload is flagged as containing synthetic media and the
description says so in plain words.

## Audio

Score and effects are deterministic synthesis written for this project. No
third-party recordings, no library stingers, no rights to clear.
