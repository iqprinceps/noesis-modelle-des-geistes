On the table beside you is a text written thirty-seven years ago by a woman who is still alive. Sealed. Carried across a continent. Read by two of your predecessors, and explained by neither.

You are about to open it.

Are you looking for the world in there?

Or are you looking for yourself?

Leave one word below: WORLD or MYSELF.

Hold on to your answer, because it decides how you read the next part.

The Vatican published the manuscript in the year 2000, as a photographic facsimile with a translation and a commentary.

So we can read exactly what he read.

Read it the way he had to. From a hospital bed. Two months after somebody shot you.

The vision opens with an angel holding a flaming sword. The flames look as though they will set the world on fire, and they go out.

Then the children see a bishop dressed in white.

They write that they had the impression this was the Holy Father.

He walks through a large city, half of it in ruins.

He passes bodies on the way.

He climbs a steep mountain with other bishops, priests and lay people, and at the top there is a large cross.
