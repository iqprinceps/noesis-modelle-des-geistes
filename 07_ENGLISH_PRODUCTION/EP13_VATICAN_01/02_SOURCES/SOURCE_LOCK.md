## The one open provenance claim

**C15 is the weakest statement in the episode, and it is in the payoff.**

The German heroasset lock recorded 1989 as established. It is not. SHR confirms
only that the crown contains the bullet, with no date, no donor and no account of
how it arrived. The regional tourism description of the crown likewise names the
bullet without a date.

Consistent secondary reporting says the Pope gave a bullet recovered from the
jeep to the Bishop of Leiria-Fatima in 1984, and that the bishop had it set into
the crown in 1989. That account is stable across sources but none of them is the
Shrine or the Holy See.

**Decision for voice lock.** The script keeps the conservative form already in
place: one of the projectiles from the attack was given to the shrine, and in
1989 it was set into the crown. It does not state where the projectile was
recovered, who carried it, or that it is the bullet that struck him.

Two hardening options, in order of preference:

1. obtain a Shrine or Holy See statement carrying the 1989 setting, then
   optionally add the 1984 handover to the Bishop of Leiria-Fatima, which is a
   strong human beat currently left out;
2. if no primary source is found, replace `In 1989 it was set into` with a form
   that does not date the setting.

Do not add the jeep. The recovery location is the specific point on which the
sources are least consistent, and the episode does not need it.

## Applied script changes

1. **Angel corrected to the source image.**
   Old: `The vision opens with an angel holding a flaming sword, pointing down at the earth.`
   New: `The vision opens with an angel holding a flaming sword. The flames look as though they will set the world on fire, and they go out.`
   Reason: VAT describes flames that appeared to set the world on fire and died
   out on contact with the light from Our Lady. The earlier line invented a
   gesture that is not in the text.

2. **Sodano quotation aligned to the published translation.**
   Old: `that a maternal hand had guided the path of the bullet.`
   New: `that it was a mother's hand that guided the bullet's path.`
   Reason: VAT renders it as a mother's hand guiding the bullet's path. The
   episode attributes this to Sodano reporting the pope's conviction, which is
   how VAT frames it.

## Deliberately not used

- The angels beneath the cross gathering the martyrs' blood in crystal vessels.
  It is in the source and it is a strong image, but the reveal act ends on the
  deaths in order to land `the bishop in white does not survive`. Available for
  the visual treatment without narration.
- `Penance, Penance, Penance!` The angel's cry is in the source and is left out
  to keep the reveal moving.
- The 1984 handover to the Bishop of Leiria-Fatima, pending C15.
