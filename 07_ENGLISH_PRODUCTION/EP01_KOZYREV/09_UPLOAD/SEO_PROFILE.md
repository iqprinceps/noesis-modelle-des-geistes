## Viewer intent

The likely cold viewer has encountered claims about a Soviet time machine and
wants either an explanation, a documentary account or evidence that the Kozyrev
Mirrors experiment was real. The episode's strongest distinctive promise is the
documented contradiction: Kozyrev did not build the patented machine carrying
his name.
