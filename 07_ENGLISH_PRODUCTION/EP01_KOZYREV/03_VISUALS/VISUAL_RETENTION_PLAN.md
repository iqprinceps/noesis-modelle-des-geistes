# EP01_EN Visual Retention Plan

## Candidate package — no creative quotas

- Shot, still, archive, card, map, and reserve counts are determined only after
  voice alignment and cue-sheet coverage review.
- The 9 named motion concepts are candidates. Concepts may be merged or removed.
- Abstract imagery is used only where it makes a spoken hypothesis materially
  clearer or more affecting.
- No asset is generated only to satisfy a quantity.
- strongest emphasis: real patent, physical scale, clean test, missing replication

The German edit repeats the chamber too often. The English edit uses the
spiral as a recurring object whose meaning changes: mystery, engineering,
claimed field, blinded test, and unresolved machine.

## Candidate motion concepts

1. `KZ_EN_CLIP01_CHAMBER_MEMORY` — reveal the chamber; the reflection briefly lags behind the participant.
2. `KZ_EN_CLIP02_PATENT_ASSEMBLY` — panels assemble, then change direction and rotate around the focus.
3. `KZ_EN_CLIP03_INTERNAL_TIME` — dust, breath, and droplets follow incompatible time directions.
4. `KZ_EN_CLIP04_DISTANT_IMAGE` — a remote landscape appears only as fractured metal reflections.
5. `KZ_EN_CLIP05_THREE_THEORIES` — one room transforms through isolation, physical change, and information access.
6. `KZ_EN_CLIP06_CLAIM_ESCALATION` — treatment becomes perception, distant information, then time.
7. `KZ_EN_CLIP07_BLIND_TARGET_TEST` — selection, participant, four targets, and blinded judging in one sequence.
8. `KZ_EN_CLIP08_REPLICATION_CHAIN` — first lab lights; independent links remain dark.
9. `KZ_EN_CLIP09_EMPTY_SPIRAL` — chamber stays still while its shadow appears to rotate.

caustics, and spatial folding. Avoid cheap glowing skin, rainbow auras, laser-like
