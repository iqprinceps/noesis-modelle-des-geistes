## Delivery

- International English, warm investigative register, never campy or credulous.
- Opening question: direct and curious, not ominous.
- Patent title and dates: clean factual authority.
- Extraordinary claims: slightly reduced intensity and explicit attribution.
- Viewer target test: inviting but procedural, no game-show energy.
- Missing replication: slow by roughly five percent with one intentional near-silent hold before `The independent result is.`
- Fort Meade handoff: forward motion, not a trailer voice.

The TTS source may contain pronunciation substitutions while the canonical script,
alignment, subtitles, and cue sheet retain correct orthography.
