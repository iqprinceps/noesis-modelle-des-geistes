# EP01_EN Editorial Map

## Promise

A rotating medical chamber is real. Three explanations compete for what happens
inside it, and one obvious experiment should separate them.

## Eight-act map

1. **Why did it rotate?** — object, moon, direction, contradictory patent title.
2. **The wrong name** — Kozyrev died before Kaznacheev and Trofimov filed the patent.
3. **The machine** — dimensions, focus, two spiral directions, motorized platform.
4. **The escalation** — treatment becomes distant information and altered time.
5. **Three interpretations** — isolation, ordinary physical change, or information access.
6. **The viewer's target** — audience chooses before the blinded protocol is revealed.
7. **The missing result** — independent replication is the absent piece.
8. **The patent trap** — the machine is real; the extraordinary effect remains open.

## First-minute anchors

- 0–5 s: full chamber emerges; motorized rotation begins.
- 5–12 s: clockwise/counterclockwise states and lunar shadow.
- 12–18 s: original patent cover and literal title translation.
- 18–30 s: three possible readings, then Kozyrev identity break.
- 30–60 s: 1983/1996 contradiction and panel assembly.

## Interaction and handoff

- Midpoint: viewer chooses one of four hidden target images.
- Final open loop: `WHY CAN'T WE SEE THE DECISIVE TEST?`
- Handoff: the unresolved protocol enters Fort Meade in EP02.
