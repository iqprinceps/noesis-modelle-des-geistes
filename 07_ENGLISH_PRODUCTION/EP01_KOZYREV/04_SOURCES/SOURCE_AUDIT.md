# EP01 Source and Rights Audit

Reviewed: 2026-08-26

## Outcome

The episode's factual spine is supported by locally archived original documents:
the Russian patent, the 2006 and 2008 Kaznacheev/Trofimov papers, authentic
Kozyrev portrait metadata, authentic Kaznacheev and Trofimov identity sources,
and public-domain Natural Earth map data.

The patent confirms filing priority on 2 July 1996, publication on 27 November
1998, inventors V. P. Kaznacheev and A. V. Trofimov, aluminum-alloy panels up to
2.8 m high and 1.2 m wide, polished inner surfaces, a proposed 0.5 m focus,
cylinder and left/right spiral configurations, and a motorized rotating platform.
Its descriptive text also favors new/full-moon periods and what it calls
magneto-ionospheric storms. These are inventor claims, not independent evidence.

Kozyrev's 1959 portrait is CC0. The Kaznacheev memorial portrait, Trofimov
conference profile, journal articles, and patent pages are retained-copyright or
official-document excerpts. Their use is limited to short, necessary,
source-identified documentary quotation. They are not decorative stock coverage,
and their publishers remain listed in video credits.

The no-repeat cut also uses three distinct Iofe archive contexts for Kozyrev
(portrait, 1989 press clipping, 1998 press clipping) and separate biographical
portraits for Kaznacheev and Trofimov from the Novosibirsk local-history
encyclopedia and Russian Cosmist Society. These files authenticate identity and
context, but no explicit commercial reuse license was located. They therefore
remain `LEGAL_REVIEW` documentary quotations, not blanket-cleared decorative
assets. The invalid black Trofimov placeholder was moved to `04_SOURCES/REJECTED`
and is excluded from the manifest.

## Editorial boundary

No located source supplies a convincing chain of independent blinded replications
for remote or future information. The absence claim in the narration is therefore
framed narrowly: the sources behind this episode do not provide that chain.

No synthetic historical document or readable pseudo-text is permitted. Generated
reconstructions never substitute for a portrait, patent page, publication, or
named person's identity beat.

The earlier German K04/K05 patent assets remain `REFERENCE_ONLY/AMBER` and are
not cleared or copied into the English timeline. The English source package uses
the newly downloaded official patent PDF. If the full page is too dense for the
hook, the edit uses a truthful metadata/source anchor and a verified original-line
crop instead of forcing the complete page on screen.
