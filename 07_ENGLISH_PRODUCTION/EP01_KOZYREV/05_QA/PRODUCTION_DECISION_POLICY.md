# EP01 Production Decision Policy

Status: binding production override, 2026-08-26

Every asset decision is evaluated from the viewer's perspective:

- immediate comprehension of the spoken beat;
- retention value and unanswered-question momentum;
- episode-specific mystery without evidentiary overstatement;
- exact semantic voice/image fit;
- visual and technical quality;
- rights confidence, generation cost, and reliability.

There are no creative quotas. Named counts and the nine motion concepts are a
candidate pool. Alignment may combine or remove them. Two short identical-text
voice auditions choose one voice/settings profile; only that profile receives a
full master. Pickups are targeted, not alternate complete versions.

The authoritative order is source/right audit, script/hook audit, short auditions,
selected full master, forced alignment, cue sheet, then required assets.
