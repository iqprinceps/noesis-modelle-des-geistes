Everyone calls this a Kozyrev Mirror.

But Kozyrev never built it.

The patent for this rotating aluminum chamber was filed thirteen years later — by two other researchers.

It describes curved panels.

A clockwise or counterclockwise spiral.

Even a motorized platform.

And it links sessions to the moon and geomagnetic storms.

The design is documented.

What happened inside remains an open question.

And that makes the legend stranger: the patent is real, but it carries the names of two other researchers.
