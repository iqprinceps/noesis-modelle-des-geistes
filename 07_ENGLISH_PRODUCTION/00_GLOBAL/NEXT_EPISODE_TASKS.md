# Episode production handoff — adaptable reference

The optimization package is a starting hypothesis, not a frozen creative lock.
For cost control and clear continuity, a production task normally owns one
episode. A different grouping is allowed when it improves the result without
creating voice, asset or review ambiguity.

## Recommended task order

1. `EP01_EN — Kozyrev Mirrors`
2. `EP02_EN — Gateway Process`
3. `EP03_EN — PEAR`
4. `EP04A_EN — Jung and Kundalini`

This order validates the new visual system on the episode needing the largest
rebuild, then carries the learned motion/card language forward.

## Core inputs

- canonical `01_SCRIPT/VOICE_SCRIPT_EN.txt`;
- `01_SCRIPT/EDITORIAL_MAP.md`;
- `03_VISUALS/VISUAL_RETENTION_PLAN.md`;
- `03_VISUALS/PREPRODUCTION_VISUAL_LOCK.md`;
- current global visual, card, and retention standards;
- German master as reference, never as a shot-by-shot template.

## Typical production outputs

Choose the outputs the episode actually needs. The lists below describe a
complete production package, not a creative quantity gate.

### Voice

- pronunciation sheet for all names, dates, acronyms, and technical terms;
- short English voice auditions when the narrator or performance is not already
  fixed by series continuity;
- one complete master only after the narrator and settings are selected;
- selected clean master plus pickups;
- word- or sentence-level timing alignment;
- loudness-normalized narration stem without music.

### Visuals

- exact archive/source/license manifest;
- geometry or character continuity notes where they add value;
- every cue-sheet-required hero still and reconstruction; no quantity generation;
- only the cards actually selected by the aligned cue sheet, in editable states;
- the episode's cue-sheet-selected motion clips with enough frame proof to review
  the intended transformation;
- dedicated abstract/mystical sequences in the episode's own visual grammar;
- an opening review artifact when it helps compare the hook states;
- mobile readability proof and visual-distinction audit without persistent mode badges;
- enough meaningfully different thumbnail compositions to support a real choice.

### Assembly readiness

- voice-aligned cue sheet;
- asset manifest mapping every spoken beat to a primary and fallback visual;
- no duplicated asset IDs, missing sources, fake readable text, or unresolved disclosure issues;
- no asset returns after a visual has left the timeline; adjacent cues may share
  one continuous shot, but later repetition is a picture-lock failure;
- updated series-wide asset-usage registry with content hashes; every final visual
  belongs to one English episode only;
- delivery into the existing episode directories only.

## Generation order inside each later task

1. source/rights and canonical-script timing audit;
2. two short voice auditions and narrator/settings selection;
3. one complete voice master, pickups, and word alignment;
4. aligned cue sheet plus the opening visual package;
5. continuity review, then required archive/still package;
6. required core motion and abstract package; no stretch clip without a cue-sheet gap;
7. selected cards, mobile proof, and final asset audit;
8. handoff for timeline, sound design, and render.

Earlier optimization did not commit the later tasks to any asset or voice quantity.
