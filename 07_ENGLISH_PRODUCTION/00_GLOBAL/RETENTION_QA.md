## Episode-by-episode script review lenses

- An episode-specific contradiction opens before biography or explanation.
- The evidence/reality anchor lands at the moment that best strengthens this
  episode's hook and trust.
- The hook promises a later payoff that the middle delivers.
- Every act changes the governing question rather than merely adding facts.
- Interaction asks the viewer to decide inside the story.
- The verified question resolves before speculative interpretations reopen.
- Final lines transform the current mechanism into the next episode's premise.
- Any subscribe request follows the story payoff and continues the episode's
  specific investigation rather than interrupting evidence.

## Visual review outcomes

- English versions are new picture edits, not dubbed copies.
- Every German visual mode has `KEEP`, `REWORK`, or `RETIRE` guidance.
- Cards show one claim, number, or question and remain mobile-readable.
- Each episode has an intentionally chosen set of motion concepts, including
  abstract/mystical candidates. Alignment may consolidate or remove them; there
  is no quota-driven generation.
- Authentic sources establish reality; `INNER / HYPOTHESIS` makes the possibility felt.
- First minute uses meaningful changes rather than a fixed shot quota.
- Visual rhythm is reviewed for the moment attention begins to settle; there is
  no universal mode-change interval.
- Subjective imagery has a distinct visual grammar without permanent mode labels.
- The timeline contains zero non-contiguous returns of the same still, document,
  card, clip state, or perceptually near-identical derivative.
- Static holds normally sit around 3–6 seconds. Every hold from roughly 8 seconds
  receives a viewer review; an unjustified hold beyond roughly 10 seconds is revised.

## Status and later production reviews

Script, viewer logic, visual language, cards, mystical image direction, core clip
scope, and thumbnail direction remain revisable when voice alignment or a viewer
review exposes a stronger solution. Assets and voices belong to single-episode
production tasks.

Each later episode uses the smallest useful set of checks to protect these
outcomes. The method may change when another review is more informative:

1. exact source/license list for reused archive;
2. pronunciation sheet and chosen English voice;
3. timed voice read for cue timestamps;
4. asset manifest with prompt IDs and lineage;
5. opening review before avoidable bulk generation;
6. mobile-card, visual-distinction, and motion-transformation checks where relevant.
7. asset-ID, content-hash, and perceptual-hash repeat audit; every non-contiguous
   return is corrected before picture lock.
8. longest-static-hold report with a short viewer rationale for each hold at or
   above 8 seconds.
