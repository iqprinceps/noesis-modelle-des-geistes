# Viewer Retention Review — Preproduction Hypotheses

Reviewed: 2026-08-26

These scores are editorial predictions, not performance data. They compare the
German picture language and earlier English drafts with the current English concept.
Real validation begins with thumbnail tests and audience-retention graphs.

## EP01 — Kozyrev Mirrors

The old viewer journey had a strong object but became technical too early. Repeated
chamber images and dense patent cards made the middle feel complete before the
decisive question arrived.

The current version opens with `Why did a medical device need to rotate?`, promises
three interpretations, makes moon/direction/magnetosphere increasingly strange,
lets the viewer choose a hidden target, and pays off with the absent independent
result. The Fort Meade handoff converts that unresolved test into EP02.

| Dimension | Earlier draft | Current concept |
|---|---:|---:|
| First 30 seconds | 6/10 | 9/10 |
| Mid-episode curiosity | 5/10 | 8/10 |
| Interaction quality | 5/10 | 8/10 |
| Next-episode pull | 4/10 | 9/10 |

Production risk: inconsistent chamber geometry or repeated reconstructions can
make the mystery feel synthetic. EP01 therefore benefits from strong visual continuity.

## EP02 — Gateway

The old opening was already strong, but repeated dossier pages flattened the middle.
The leap from sound to cosmology was explained rather than experienced.

The current version stages Recommendation H before revealing the source, promises the
ten-digit result in the hook, makes the viewer feel the crossing from acoustics into
cosmology, and asks them to choose `AUTHORIZE` or `STOP`. Abstract clips let the
viewer enter Focus states before the official page pulls them back to evidence.

Production risk: too many static documents will turn the film back into a lesson.
Source reveals should isolate a consequential line and leave before the evidence
feels like a lesson; motion is one option, not a required reset.

## EP03 — PEAR

The old version delayed Operator 10 until after a long machine explanation. Complete
infographic cards made the tiny anomaly clear but removed suspense.

The current version names Operator 10 immediately, makes the viewer search for one
changed bit, and turns every statistical detail into a threat to the result. The
audience chooses the precise point at which it would close the lab. The mystical
layer is mathematical: intention deforms noise and appears on the wrong side of time.

## EP04A — Jung and Kundalini

This was already the best German visual baseline. Its weaker passages became
instructional: emotion-regulation cards and chakra genealogy interrupted the uncanny
inner journey.

The current version opposes Jung's warning with his own serpent, makes `projection or
recurring structure?` the engine, and reveals that the map itself changed while
traveling. It has the richest abstract motion package and ends with the identity
question `MAP` or `MIRROR`, followed by Pauli and coincidence.

## Series follower strategy

The endings now hand off an unresolved mechanism rather than advertise the channel:

`chamber` → `military protocol` → `machine data` → `autonomous images` → `Pauli and coincidence`

This gives viewers a reason to follow the investigation. A brief end-screen prompt
may appear only after the next-file promise has landed.

## Metrics to validate after production

- thumbnail click-through rate by concept, not color variation;
- 0–30 second retention and the drop before the first source reveal;
- retention at every full-screen card and every document cluster;
- rewatches around rotation, ten digits, Operator 10, and six centers;
- comment conversion on each episode-specific decision;
- end-screen continuation into the next file;
- new subscribers per unique viewer, compared episode by episode.
