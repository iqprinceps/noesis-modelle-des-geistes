## Current target

Aim for two strong longforms per calendar week while the ready-master inventory
supports it. A third longform is useful only when the films are genuinely ready
and the release does not compress a continuing series or make its own support
Shorts compete with it. This is a planning target, not permission to lower
episode quality or publish unfinished work.

Shorts serve a specific longform and should normally occupy the days between
long releases. Avoid two new uploads close together on the same day unless
channel data gives an episode-specific reason.

## Pre-publication review

For each individual film, verify the actual YouTube state rather than relying
on a local manifest:

- channel ID and handle;
- processed master and intended duration;
- title/thumbnail complement rather than duplication;
- description opening, search phrase and episode-specific interaction;
- custom thumbnail at full resolution and mobile readability;
- manual English captions in serving state;
- language, category, child setting and synthetic-media disclosure where
  applicable;
- correct series playlist and any native related-video link available after the
  longform becomes public;
- no same-day release collision that weakens the first audience test.

Publication hours and weekly density remain testable decisions. Update them
when the English channel has enough impression, geography and retention data;
do not turn the current times into permanent creative locks.
