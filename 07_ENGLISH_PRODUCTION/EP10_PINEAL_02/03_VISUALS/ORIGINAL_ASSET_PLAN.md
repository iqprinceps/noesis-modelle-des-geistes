## Rights / provenance rules

- Original historical portraits and documents are preferred wherever they exist.
- Generated images may reconstruct rooms, hands, mechanisms and transitions, but never masquerade as archival evidence.
- Every downloaded binary gets source URL, acquisition date, creator/institution, stated rights, checksum and local filename in the external-storage manifest.
- No modern copyrighted web illustration is to be used as decorative coverage merely because it appears in search results.
- If a scan repository imposes additional reuse terms on digitization, record those terms separately from the public-domain status of the underlying seventeenth-century work.
