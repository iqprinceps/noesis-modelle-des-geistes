## Story / retention verdict

EP10 is production-worthy in its current form. Its engine is not `pineal trivia`; it is a human confrontation. The viewer first experiences the unity problem, then watches Elisabeth force Descartes to explain the invisible causal link in his own system. The strongest retention beats are the opening eye test, the dated letter, the mechanical-body reveal, the hand-raising test, Elisabeth's second objection and the EP11 handoff.

Do not replace this with a generic history-of-philosophy rewrite.
