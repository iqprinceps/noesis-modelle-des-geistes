- **GREEN — VERIFIED/CLEARED:** landing page and content were confirmed; the stated licence/public-domain status supports production use subject to normal credit/provenance capture.

## B. The four 1643 letters — documentary proof

### Calendar/date handling

The manuscript-date convention used by the English script is **6 May 1643** and **10 June 1643** for Elisabeth’s two key letters. Adam–Tannery/Wikisource can expose dual-calendar/editorial forms **6/16 May** and **10/20 June**. Production must preserve the source metadata and must not “correct” a facsimile date to make it visually match narration. The narration follows the manuscript-date convention; the source register records the variant.

## Production rule

For every acquired binary, the external storage manifest must record: asset ID, original landing URL, final resolved download URL, acquisition UTC timestamp, institution/creator, original date, rights statement, licence URL where applicable, MIME type, pixel dimensions/page count, byte size, SHA-256, local filename, and exact edit use. Large binaries remain outside GitHub.
