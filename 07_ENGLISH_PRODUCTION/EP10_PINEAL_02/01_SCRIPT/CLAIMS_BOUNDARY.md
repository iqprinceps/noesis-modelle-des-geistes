# EP10_EN — Claim Map

## Established story anchors

- Descartes assigned the pineal gland a central role in the interaction of soul and body.
- His physiology used ventricles, nerves and animal spirits as mechanical elements.
- Elisabeth's May sixteen forty-three letters directly challenged how an immaterial soul could move a body and be affected by it.
- Descartes replied on May twenty-first and June twenty-eighth, addressing body, soul and their union.
- Elisabeth stated that granting matter and extension to the soul seemed easier than explaining immaterial bodily action.

## Attribution and open meaning

- `Seat of the soul`, pineal motion and animal-spirit flow always remain Descartes' historical model.
- Elisabeth's arguments are paraphrases, not invented quotations.
- Modern resonance concerns the relation between distributed physical processes and unified experience; it does not turn the pineal gland into a modern consciousness center.
