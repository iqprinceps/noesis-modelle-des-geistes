# EP10_EN — Editorial Map

## Viewer promise

The episode follows the exact question a young princess used to expose the
missing link inside Descartes' model of body, soul and pineal gland.

## Story movements

1. Viewer experiment: two eyes, one experienced world.
2. Elisabeth's letter of May sixth, sixteen forty-three.
3. Descartes' mechanical body and animal spirits.
4. Why a single central gland appeared useful to him.
5. Viewer raises a hand and encounters the invisible first link.
6. Descartes' May twenty-first reply.
7. Elisabeth's second objection and the June twenty-eighth answer.
8. Modern resonance: one location does not explain interaction.
9. The anatomical image migrates toward the third eye in EP11.

## Interaction

The final viewer decision asks who saw the deeper problem more clearly:
Descartes or Elisabeth. The answer requires engagement with the central conflict.

## Primary visual anchors for later production

- authentic Descartes and Elisabeth portraits;
- complete, readable letter context with exact line highlights;
- historical pineal/ventricle anatomy and period diagrams;
- a restrained reconstruction of writing and reading the correspondence;
- embodied hand-raising sequence with a visible transition from intention to motion;
- no invented portrait likeness for either historical person.
