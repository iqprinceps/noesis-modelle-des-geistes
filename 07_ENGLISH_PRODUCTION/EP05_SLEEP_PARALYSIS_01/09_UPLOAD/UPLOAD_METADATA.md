## Title

Sleep Paralysis: Why You Feel Someone in the Room

## Description

Sleep paralysis can leave the body measurably immobilized while the mind is awake. But why does the state so often feel like another presence has entered the room?

This investigative mystery documentary follows folklorist David Hufford from one frightening night to Newfoundland's Old Hag tradition, the measurable REM-atonia contradiction, J. Allan Cheyne's three recurring experience families, Takeuchi's 1992 sleep-interruption study, and a night that entered the Salem record.

The paralysis is measurable. Why it can feel like another awareness is present remains the question.

Which came first for you: PRESSURE, SOUND, or PRESENCE? Leave one word below — and, if you have experienced it, tell us what happened first.
