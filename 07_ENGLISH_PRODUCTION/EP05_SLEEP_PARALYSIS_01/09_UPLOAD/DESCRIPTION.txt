Sleep paralysis can leave you awake, unable to move, and certain that someone—or something—is in the room. Why do people across cultures describe a presence, a shadow figure, or what is often called a “sleep paralysis demon”?

This mystery documentary follows folklorist David Hufford from one frightening night to Newfoundland’s Old Hag tradition, the measurable REM-atonia mechanism, J. Allan Cheyne’s three recurring experience families, Takeuchi’s 1992 sleep-interruption study, and a night that entered the Salem record.

The body’s paralysis is measurable. The presence is the unanswered part.

Which came first for you: PRESSURE, SOUND, or PRESENCE? Leave one word below—and, if you have experienced it, tell us what happened first.

Subscribe to follow Part Two, where a private night experience enters the Salem courtroom and becomes evidence against another person.
