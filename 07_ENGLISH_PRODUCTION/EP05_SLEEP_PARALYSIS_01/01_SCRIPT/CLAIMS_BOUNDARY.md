# EP05_EN Claims Boundary

This file protects factual meaning without freezing pacing, wording or visual
form. The current English owner decisions and episode-specific viewer experience
take precedence over legacy formatting rules.

## Supported anchors

- Sleep paralysis combines waking awareness with persisting REM-related muscle
  atonia.
- David Hufford described a 1963 Old-Hag-like experience before knowing the
  Newfoundland tradition.
- Hufford reported comparable accounts from people who did not know that
  tradition beforehand.
- J. Allan Cheyne and colleagues described recurring Intruder, Incubus and
  unusual bodily-experience clusters.
- The 1992 Takeuchi sleep-interruption study produced six isolated
  sleep-paralysis episodes under the experimental conditions.
- The Takeuchi paper reports a REM/wake dissociated state with persistent muscle
  atonia on polysomnography, alongside participants' awareness of lying in the
  laboratory.
- Richard Coman's June 2, 1692 testimony attributed repeated nocturnal pressure,
  speechlessness and immobility to Bridget Bishop. The film presents this as his
  recorded allegation, not as proof that Bishop entered his room.
- Bridget Bishop was tried and convicted in June 1692 and executed on June 10.

## Open territory

- The experience does not establish an external entity.
- A threat-prediction or agency-detection account is presented as one plausible
  route, not as the final explanation of every episode.
- The film may visualize a presence boldly and mystically. It must not present
  that image as documentary footage or as proof of a being.
- The feedback loop between physiology, experience and folklore is the episode's
  closing theory, not a settled causal model.

## Language approach

Avoid dismissive constructions such as `just a hallucination`, `nothing but a
dream`, or repeated `not proof` cards. State what the evidence positively
establishes, then name the question it leaves open.

## Synchronization rule

Voice, source, card, image, SFX and later summary must preserve the same
evidentiary strength. A card may not become more skeptical or more categorical
than the narration. Subjective footsteps, pressure and presence may be embodied
in sound, but SFX must not certify an external visitor. The film states the
measurable mechanism positively, then opens only the question the mechanism does
not settle.
