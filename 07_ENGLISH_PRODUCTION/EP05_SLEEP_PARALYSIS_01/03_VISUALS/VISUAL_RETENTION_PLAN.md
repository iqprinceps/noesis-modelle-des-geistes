# EP05_EN Visual Retention Plan — Sleep Paralysis I

This episode receives a new English picture journey. The German master is a
reference for strengths and failure modes, not a reusable timeline.

## Viewer-side diagnosis of the German master

- The Hufford → Old Hag opening and Salem handoff are the strongest movements.
- The REM section becomes visually instructional.
- The three experience families are presented as taxonomy instead of being felt.
- The laboratory section clusters too many static source shots.
- Twenty-two underlying images return later in the timeline.
- Six clips provide only about 23 seconds of real motion; a roughly 2.5-minute
  stretch contains no genuine moving-image beat.
- Several disclaimer cards weaken the mystery and resemble slides.

## Working coverage assumption — not a quota

Final counts follow the aligned voice and cue sheet. For an expected 9–10-minute
master, the current planning range is:

- roughly 115–130 meaningful, non-returning picture states;
- 24–32 authentic archive, place, science and source anchors;
- 48–58 new 16:9 reconstruction or abstract/mystical stills;
- 8–12 minimal cards or progressive explanatory states;
- 5–6 core motion clips, with up to two reserve concepts generated only if the
  aligned edit exposes a real coverage gap.

No still, document, card or clip state may return after the timeline leaves it.
A person, room or idea may recur only through a genuinely new source, viewpoint,
action or advanced state.

## Six core motion concepts

1. **The 1963 approach** — door opens, steps draw closer, mattress compresses.
   One escalating action; no creature reveal.
2. **The interrupted command** — awareness reaches the room while the motor
   command visibly fails before reaching the body.
3. **Three shapes of the night** — Intruder becomes Incubus, then the viewpoint
   separates from the body in one progressive transformation.
4. **The Takeuchi sequence** — sleep, timed awakening, one hour awake, return to
   bed, then six distinct measured events.
5. **Presence before form** — occupied negative space gains direction and
   intention before any incomplete human outline appears.
6. **Bedroom to courtroom** — a private night transforms into Salem testimony,
   ending on the archival/legal consequence rather than a generic witch image.

Veo is reserved for these internal transformations or unavailable actions.
Atmospheric drift by itself does not justify a generation.

## Image language

- Use physically plausible rooms, pressure, fabric deformation, peripheral
  vision, breath condensation, negative space and incomplete agency.
- Abstract imagery should make the supernatural interpretation emotionally
  visible without settling whether an entity exists.
- Avoid neon brains, generic meditation stock, sterile blue-gold corridors,
  fantasy monsters, repeated shadow figures and fake readable research text.
- Documents receive full readable context and one consequential highlight. The
  camera never crops through the line being discussed.
- Cards are minimal visual decisions, not numbered lecture slides.
- No permanent `RECONSTRUCTION`, `HYPOTHESIS` or similar badges in the film.

## Motion and technical QA

- Still motion uses the shared eased high-resolution motion function, with
  smooth sub-frame interpolation; no local low-resolution `zoompan` recreation.
- 23.976/24 fps generated clips convert to 30 fps through motion-aware
  interpolation after visual inspection, not duplicated-frame `fps=30` cadence.
- A slow zoom or pan does not rescue an empty hold. Review every static or nearly
  static hold around eight seconds and above from the viewer's side.
- Final QA includes rendered-camera smoothness, cadence, full timeline contact
  sheet, source readability, perceptual duplicate audit and zero
  non-contiguous asset returns.
