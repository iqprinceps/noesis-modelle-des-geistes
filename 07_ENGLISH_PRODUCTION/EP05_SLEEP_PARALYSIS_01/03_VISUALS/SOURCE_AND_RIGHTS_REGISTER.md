## Excluded / provenance-only variants

- duplicate download of the Bishop execution record;
- 403/429 error-page captures;
- full-page Takeuchi capture after the complete-context 16:9 card is used;
- standalone Hufford photo after the official biography-page state is used;
- all German EP06 timeline derivatives and repeated series assets.

Final selections are referenced by manifest with origin, license/use basis,
retrieval date, SHA-256, dimensions, voice beat and picture function.
