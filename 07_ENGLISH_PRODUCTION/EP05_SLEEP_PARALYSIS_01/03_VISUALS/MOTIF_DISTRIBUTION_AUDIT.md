## Viewer-side balance

- Literal bedroom/bed detail is planned for roughly one fifth of aligned cue
  groups and is concentrated where the voice is bodily. It is not the default
  visual grammar.
- A recognizable person lying in bed is not a repeating motif. Most bodily
  states use hand, fabric, breath consequence, subjective axis, motor path or
  abstract body boundary.
- Fogo/place/map, real science/lab, publication/document, abstract perception,
  interaction and Salem/history each receive sustained distinct runs.
- Transition bridges are locked as coast → measurement, PSG signal → subjective
  room, paper → protocol → source, and bedroom → testimony → courtroom.
