# EP05_EN Viewer-First Creative Direction

Status: **current episode-specific picture direction**  

This direction records the choices that served this episode. It is not a
cross-episode quota or immutable template; a later viewer review may improve it.
Date: 2026-08-27

## Visual promise

The episode is a premium mystical investigative documentary: embodied,
atmospheric and human. We live through one night, discover a repeating pattern,
enter the science, see the measurable contradiction, and follow private
experience into a public courtroom. Authentic sources provide credibility;
faces provide emotional attachment; perceived figures and transformations
provide mystery; visible mechanisms provide the “aha”.

Every viewer-facing state must do at least one of these jobs:

1. build suspense;
2. make the spoken idea visible;
3. create emotional proximity;
4. deliver a surprising reveal;
5. produce an explanatory “aha”.

An image that does none of these does not enter the final film.

## Positive picture language

- People are the emotional center: credible faces, open eyes, controlled fear,
  breath, skin, hands, gaze direction, observers and social reactions.
- Perceived beings may become visible: shadow people, incomplete humanoid
  forms, a restrained hag-like presence, bodily pressure, and an out-of-body
  double. Adult, subtle and uncanny—not camp, not a fantasy monster.
- Empty rooms are expectation only. Their immediate successor must pay off with
  movement, face, figure, pressure, trace or reveal.
- Sources are discovered cinematically: paper/material/context/hand/highlight,
  then a match cut into the lived or measured scene. Never a dry evidence slide.
- One clear focus per state: face, figure, source or mechanism—not all at once.
- Internal QA, XML, retrieval, license, path and asset metadata never appear in
  viewer-facing frames.

## Creative freedom and identity boundary

Generated/dramatized people, faces, ghosts, shadow beings, hag-like forms,
historical situations, symbolic worlds, anatomical brain/brainstem/motor-path
models and transformations are welcome when they make the voice more vivid.
Reconstruction is not treated as a visual defect and receives no permanent
label. The only identity boundary is strict: a generated person is never
presented as David Hufford, J. Allan Cheyne, a Takeuchi author or Bridget
Bishop. When a named person enters the voice, an authentic identity/publication
anchor appears first or in direct parallel. Non-identifying witnesses,
participants, researchers, villagers and historical crowds may be freely and
credibly dramatized.

Mechanism imagery may show an anatomically plausible brain, brainstem, spinal
cord and motor pathway. It must produce an immediate bodily insight—dream
activity continues, motor inhibition remains, and the command fails before the
hand—without becoming a generic neon brain or sterile lecture graphic.

## Timed visual landings

- `Old Hag` — hag-like form or historically identified iconography becomes
  recognizable, never before the phrase lands.
- `mind reaches the room before movement reaches your muscles` — awareness is
  present in eye/face; motor impulse visibly fails before the hand.
- `three broad families` — three human experience worlds transform in sequence.
- `six episodes` — exactly six discrete measurement events land on the words.
- `presence arrives before the image` — observer face reacts before the partial
  figure forms.
- `private night entered the public record` — room material becomes testimony,
  then court/social consequence.

## Rhythm and transitions

- A breathing shot still contains discovery, action or meaning change.
- Smooth/eased still motion only. Human/action/transformation beats get the few
  justified clips; atmosphere alone does not.
- Match cuts carry meaning: eye→EOG, mattress depression→trace trough,
  coastal fog→hag, paper mark→lab, partial figure→court silhouette.
- No arbitrary slideshow dissolve. No long static emptiness without payoff.
- Faces and bodies can recur as motif families only through new people,
  viewpoints, actions or later states; underlying images never return.

## Sound as visible material

Door mechanics, two spatially distinct steps, fabric/mattress compression,
controlled breath, electrode/relay clicks, coastal wind, pencil/ink and subdued
court murmur are attached to visible events. No cheap horror hits and no sound
that asserts an external entity as fact.

## Gate

Before each generation batch, verify: what does the viewer feel or understand;
where is the human anchor; what is revealed; what moves; why does it look worth
watching? Before Picture Lock, repeat the face/body/figure/empty-space audit and
reject any sequence that has become a slideshow of rooms, beds, cards or labs.
