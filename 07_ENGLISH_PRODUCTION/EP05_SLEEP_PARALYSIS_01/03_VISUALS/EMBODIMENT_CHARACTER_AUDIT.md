## Freedom lock

All corrective characters and perceived beings may be newly generated. They do
not need a permanent reconstruction badge. The constraint is not “avoid
figures”; it is “do not counterfeit a named identity or historical source.”
Non-identifying 1963 sleepers, Newfoundland witnesses/villagers, laboratory
participants/researchers, shadow people, hag-like presences, body doubles,
Salem witnesses and court crowds are approved creative subjects. Authentic
Hufford/Cheyne/Takeuchi/Bishop anchors remain adjacent whenever the voice names
them.
