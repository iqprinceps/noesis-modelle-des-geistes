# EP05 EN — Audio Semantic Cue Plan

## Mix intent

Voice is the perceptual center. Music changes material and density with the five visual worlds; it is never a permanent horror drone. Subjective sounds embody reported pressure, steps, breath, space, and transition without asserting an external entity. Science receives cleaner transients and more silence. `Presence before form` receives controlled emptiness and directed proximity, not a jump-scare hit.

## Ducking and silence gates

- Music sidechain: medium release so sentences remain intimate without audible pumping.
- SFX sidechain: faster release; transients sit behind consonants and never mask the named event.
- The deepest reduction is 362.72–377.68, where certainty precedes visible form.
- No broadband hit is placed on a claim about a visitor, witch, or entity.
- Final target: **-14 LUFS integrated ±0.5**, **true peak ≤ -0.8 dBTP**, 48 kHz stereo.
