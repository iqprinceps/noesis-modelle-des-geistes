# EP05_EN Voice Production Plan

Voice generation starts only after the channel owner has read the canonical
script aloud or approved it for audition. This prevents generating a full master
from wording that will still change.

## Flow

1. Build a pronunciation sheet for Hufford, Fogo, Cheyne, Takeuchi, REM atonia,
   Intruder, Incubus and Bridget Bishop.
2. Generate two short auditions from the same representative excerpt: the
   Hufford cold open followed by `This explains the paralysis. It does not yet
   explain the footsteps.`
3. Compare intimacy, intelligibility, natural pauses and unease. Do not compare
   different words or different mixes.
4. Generate one full selected master.
5. Listen through against the script and make only targeted pickups.
6. Force-align the approved master. Visual cue timing derives from that
   alignment, never from estimated reading speed.

## Delivery direction

- calm, intimate international English;
- the narrator is sharing a disturbing case, not presenting a lecture;
- short sentences may breathe; avoid trailer-style overacting;
- science becomes clearer and slightly faster, then slows at `presence before
  form`;
- Hufford's report and all theories must sound curious, never mocking.
