# Gateway Short — Viewer Retention Plan

This short is a self-contained decision story built around Recommendation H.

- Opening promise: the familiar CIA label plus a test viewers probably have not heard.
- Rapid escalation: target, present, past, future, comparison.
- Source reveal: authentic 1983 Army report and Recommendation H.
- Reversal: the document specifies the test while leaving its outcome unresolved.
- Interaction: `AUTHORIZE` or `STOP` is part of the story, not a generic CTA.
- Conversion: the longform resolves authorship, mechanism and evidentiary gap.

Visuals move in one direction with no asset returns. The exact cut rhythm follows
the selected voice alignment rather than a fixed shot duration.
