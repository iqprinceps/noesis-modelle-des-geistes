# EP02 Gateway — Preproduction Visual Lock

## Episode override: viewer-facing labels

The mode names `ARCHIVE`, `ORIGINAL DOCUMENT`, `RECONSTRUCTION`, `MODEL`, and
`INNER / HYPOTHESIS` are internal production metadata only. They are never
permanent viewer-facing badges in EP02_EN.

- Archive portraits carry no mode badge; source and license live in the manifest
  and video credits.
- The first relevant original-document proof may use one concise source/date line
  or a targeted highlight of the authentic line. No persistent mode label follows.
- Reconstruction is communicated primarily through the episode's distinct,
  consistent visual grammar. At the first entry into a continuous reconstruction
  block only, an optional discreet contextual line may appear for 1.5–2.0 seconds.
- Abstract and mystical material carries no visible production-category label.
- Cue-sheet, manifest, and QA metadata still record the mode for every asset and
  shot so evidentiary boundaries remain auditable.

This episode-specific viewer-label rule supersedes conflicting global and older
German production wording.

## Binding linear no-repeat override

The final aligned edit is a one-way visual progression. An asset export is used
in one contiguous block and never returns after a cut. Alternate crops, zooms,
pans, overlays, or grades of that export are not valid callbacks. Later mentions
use a different authenticated detail, source context, action, composition, or a
genuinely advanced diagram state. Clips play through a single non-restarted
source interval. This rule also forbids reuse of EP01 English final exports.

Static shots normally remain within 3–6 seconds; 8 seconds triggers review and
over 10 seconds requires visible internal development plus a written viewer
reason. Quantities are diagnostic, never quotas. Final QA must report edit shots,
asset runs, unique semantic motifs, longest static hold, all holds at or above
8 seconds, exact/near hashes, and intra-/cross-episode repeat counts.

## Viewer diagnosis of the German material

This is already the strongest document-thriller premise: Army letterhead,
headphones, numbered mental states, and impossible recommendations. The weakness
is repetition of mode. Full pages, dark green information cards, blurred diagrams,
and abstract field imagery make the middle feel like a dossier being summarized
instead of a plan becoming increasingly dangerous.

The English edit must make Recommendation H physically unfold.

## Keep / rework / retire

### KEEP

- original Army report pages and CIA archive provenance;
- authentic Monroe, Bentov, McDonnell, Fort Meade, and Flight 191 archive;
- best restrained headphones/laboratory reconstructions;
- language-neutral Focus-level and binaural-beat explanatory elements.

### REWORK

- full pages become tight line reveals with page context first and readable crop second;
- the Focus ladder becomes a continuous map that crosses a threshold at Focus 15;
- the ten-digit test becomes a procedural scene, not a result card;
- Recommendation H builds one observer at a time across a moving target line.

### RETIRE

- dark status dashboards and central-question cards;
- generic neon brains, stock portals, floating-head composites, and repeated torus imagery;
- unreadable full-page documents held under narration;
- the generic `YES / NO` card;
- cards that announce an evidence verdict before the viewer sees the evidence.

## Visual story

The episode begins as an official memo. Each act adds one component to an
engineering diagram until the diagram quietly becomes a map outside time. The
visual unease comes from official language and procedural calm, not horror effects.

## Abstract / mystical layer

The viewer must temporarily enter the states the report describes. Use phase-shifted
silhouettes, an out-of-body viewpoint that rises without spectacle, wave fields
crossing into impossible spatial axes, time spokes folding around a human figure,
and a responsive presence made only from distortion and negative space. The official
page should repeatedly pull the viewer back out of the vision.

## First 60-second lock

- 0–8 s: three human observers occupy present, immediate past, and immediate future.
- 8–14 s: their incomplete descriptions overlap; target remains withheld.
- 14–20 s: hard reveal of Recommendation H on the original report.
- 20–28 s: Army recipient, date, author, and Fort Meade; CIA archive only as provenance.
- 28–38 s: the forbidden question: why was time treated as a coordinate?
- 38–48 s: Recommendation J line about nonphysical intelligence.
- 48–60 s: headphones, Monroe's laboratory, then the two tones begin.
