## Provenance distinction locked in picture and voice

The document was prepared for the U.S. Army and signed by Lieutenant Colonel
Wayne M. McDonnell. The CIA connection is the archival/declassification copy and
its release marks. No card, subtitle, cue, or narration line calls the CIA the
author. McDonnell is introduced through his authentic signature block because no
cleared, verified portrait was found; the anonymous officer reconstruction is
shown from behind and is never presented as his likeness.

Robert Monroe is introduced by name through his authentic patent record. The
following radio studio, laboratory, and training images are identity-neutral
reconstructions and do not claim to depict Monroe's likeness. Itzhak Bentov is
introduced through his authentic controlled-motion-device patent; the adjacent
catheter bench and body-oscillation scenes are likewise identity-neutral.
