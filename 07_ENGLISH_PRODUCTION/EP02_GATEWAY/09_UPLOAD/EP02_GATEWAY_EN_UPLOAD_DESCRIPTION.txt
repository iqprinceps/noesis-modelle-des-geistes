The ‘CIA Gateway Process’ explained through the original 1983 U.S. Army report: Hemi-Sync, the Gateway Experience, Focus 15, out-of-body claims, and a hidden ten-digit test across three times.

Three observers. One hidden target. One searches the present, one the immediate past, and one the immediate future. That was Recommendation H in a report prepared for the United States Army at Fort Meade. The CIA connection is the archive copy — it was not a CIA-authored report.

This documentary traces how Robert Monroe’s Hemi-Sync training, Itzhak Bentov’s speculative physical model and Lieutenant Colonel Wayne M. McDonnell’s analysis became a proposed intelligence test. At its center is a ten-digit experiment: too incomplete to prove the claim, yet apparently interesting enough for McDonnell to keep going.
