## Description

Three observers. One target. One searches the present, one the immediate past,
and one the immediate future. In 1983, Lieutenant Colonel Wayne M. McDonnell put
that proposal into Recommendation H of an analysis prepared for the U.S. Army.

This documentary follows the real paper trail behind the Gateway Process—from
Robert Monroe’s sound experiments and Itzhak Bentov’s proposed physical model to
binaural beats, Focus levels, the incomplete ten-digit test, and the report’s
claims about nonphysical presence. It also separates two facts that viral posts
often blur: the report was written for the Army; the surviving declassified copy
was later archived and released through the CIA.

The document is authentic. Its extraordinary claims are not thereby proven.

Would you authorize Recommendation H—or stop the program? Comment **AUTHORIZE**
or **STOP**.

Next: the PEAR laboratory, fourteen million trials, and the unnamed person linked
to roughly half the reported effect.
