## Creative guardrails

- Bright-readable mystical atmosphere; preserve shadow detail and midtones.
- No generic horror ghost, demon, specter or opaque black void.
- No written words, captions, logos, watermarks or pseudo-scientific labels.
- Cultural material is contextualized through coast, domestic artifacts, oral history,
  archive material and threshold symbolism; it is not presented as supernatural fact.
- Science imagery is elegant and conceptually accurate: REM atonia, motor inhibition,
  interoception, vestibular mismatch and predictive perception.
- Presence imagery uses negative space, thresholds, occlusion and perceptual geometry;
  it does not default to a figure beside a bed.
- Existing filenames remain stable. Approved replacements overwrite the listed finals.
