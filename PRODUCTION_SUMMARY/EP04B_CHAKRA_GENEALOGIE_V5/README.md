## Individual production profile

EP04B is an archive/motion detective story and therefore uses a very different production mix from EP04A:

- fewer generated images; historical originals and document details carry the strongest reveals;
- more morphs, transparent layers, route graphics and comparative motion;
- voice is split around evidence/reveal runs, not around a fixed act template;
- pauses are individually chosen starting points and remain editable after listening;
- music is dry, typographic and investigative rather than dreamlike;
- no runtime, shot count, prompt count or stem count is treated as a hard target.
