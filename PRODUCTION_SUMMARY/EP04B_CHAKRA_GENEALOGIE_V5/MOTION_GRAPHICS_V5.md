# EP04B — Motion / Graphics V5

**Principle:** EP04B's strongest visual device is transformation with the seams left visible. Real historical layers remain identifiable; motion shows relationships between them.

## B-G01 — Modern map reverse
**Anchor:** opening.  
**Motion:** modern seven-color map → rainbow color layer disappears → order loosens → hard cut to real historical plate. Do not pretend the historical plate is the direct hidden layer of one poster.

## B-G02 — Many maps
**Anchor:** `Der erste Fehler steckt schon im Singular.`

**Motion:** several source objects appear side by side at their real proportions. No forced common anatomy.

## Motion QA

- timing follows the voice and source readability, not a preset duration.
- use real historical assets at real aspect ratios; contain rather than crop away evidence.
- all added explanatory typography German.
- citations/source labels stay attached to their source objects.
- no morph that visually claims one historical system literally evolved frame-by-frame into the next.
