## Individual production profile

EP04A is not forced into Gateway/EP05 quantities or timings. Its production system follows the material:

- voice is split where the emotional/semantic performance changes, not once per act;
- pauses between voice stems are individually defined starting points and may be adjusted after listening;
- image prompts cover only shots that benefit from generation; archive and real historical material win whenever they carry the beat better;
- the visual pool is deliberately heavier on subjective reconstruction than EP04B because the episode repeatedly enters Jung's inner imagery;
- music and SFX are built around the oscillation **historical reality ↔ inner world**, not a generic mystery bed;
- no runtime is stretched or cut merely to hit a number.
