# EP04A — Motion / Graphics V5

**Principle:** archive proves, reconstruction evokes, motion clarifies. Motion never turns an open parallel into evidence.

## A-G01 — 1932 / Zürich
**Anchor:** opening sentence.  
**Motion:** brief location/year card over real Zurich/context or reconstruction. No long title sequence.

## A-G02 — Jung warning: paraphrase, not quote
**Anchor:** `Wer mit solchen Techniken ... kann sich psychisch in Gefahr bringen.`  
**Default:** modern editorial card: `JUNGS SEMINAR · ZÜRICH · 1932` + small `sinngemäß nach dem Seminarband`.  
**Rule:** use a direct quotation only if exact wording/page is separately locked. Production does not depend on a quote card.

## A-G03 — 1913 → 1914 reality snap
**Anchor:** war begins.  
**Basis:** real 1914 map/archive.  
**Motion:** date changes from `1913` to `1914`; flood texture vanishes and the real map takes over. Never imply prophecy proof.

## A-G14 — Pauli handoff
**Anchor:** final reveal.  
**Basis:** authentic Pauli archive if available/cleared.  
**Motion:** name first, then `PHYSIKER`; do not add quantum particles. Cut to black on `Problem`.

## Motion QA

- durations follow the final voice; no preset length.
- German editorial typography.
- historical original text remains original only when it is actually on the source object.
- no HUD, neon, 3D text camera or magical particle systems.
- no direct quote without exact source lock.
- no visual statement that Jung's black snake **is** Kundalini.
